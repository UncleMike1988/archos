## Refinement notes

### Tooling observations

- **OpenInsider.com is unreachable from the WebFetch tool** (ECONNREFUSED
  on both `http://openinsider.com/*` and `https://openinsider.com/*`).
  Option C primary path is broken until either (a) OpenInsider becomes
  reachable from this environment, (b) we switch to an alternative
  aggregator (WhaleWisdom, GuruFocus, insider-monitor.com), or (c) we
  accept that name-by-name EdgarTools pulls (Option B) IS the screen
  going forward. Recommend (c): EdgarTools `insider_activity` is fast,
  authoritative, and structured. The cost is having to maintain a
  candidate universe list (~50 names is the budget).
- **EdgarTools `search_filings_full_text` on Form 4 is not productive.**
  Tested with `"open market purchase" CEO president shares` over 90
  days — returned only 1 result, an unrelated financial services
  company. Form 4 XML is not text-indexed in a useful way. Skip
  Option A in future runs.
- **EdgarTools `insider_activity` summary has data-quality caveats
  on net_value calculations** — for EOSE the summary said
  `net_value: $227K` (positive) while the transaction-level sum was
  `-$693K` (the BUYS shown as negative — appears to be a sign-
  convention bug). The `transactions[]` array is reliable; the
  `summary.net_value` aggregate is not.
- **`most_recent_open_market_buy` is the single most useful field
  in the summary** — it's the cleanest indicator of whether
  insiders have done ANY open-market P-buying recently. `null` or
  "stale by 1+ year" eliminates a name immediately.
- **Foreign filers (POET / IREN / BTDR / SIVEF / 3778.T) have no
  Form 4 coverage** — this is a known structural gap. Need
  separate workflow for non-US listings if this screen is to
  reach full universe coverage.

### Signal observations

- **Phase 1 H3 lesson is empirically validated in real-time.**
  Of 39 names where Form 4 data was queryable, 38 show insiders
  SELLING (or grant-only activity) and only 1 (EOSE) shows
  cluster BUYING. The chokepoint pure-play universe currently
  has zero insiders buying. AXTI/AAOI/BE/NVTS/CRDO/AEHR all show
  the textbook H3 pattern — insiders selling AS the stock rerates.
- **The "buying into weakness" signal is rare and load-bearing
  when it fires.** EOSE is a clean example of the signal mechanics
  (CEO + 2 directors, cluster, deep drawdown, $693K aggregate) —
  but the H11 filter correctly catches the optic-creation risk.
- **Zero clean PASS candidates in this run is the right outcome.**
  Same calibration as bellwether sweep (zero-hit weeks) and
  revenue-inflection (zero strict passes in first 2 runs). Tight
  specificity is the feature. Monthly cadence justified.
