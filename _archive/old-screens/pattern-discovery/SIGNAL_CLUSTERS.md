# SIGNAL_CLUSTERS.md — Named Pattern Bundles from Phase 3 Universal Discovery

**Date:** 2026-05-26
**Companion files:** WINNER_UNIVERSE.md (universe), PATTERN_MATRIX_UNIVERSAL.md (per-signal matrix), NEW_SCREENS_SPEC.md (forward screens), batch1-5_*.md (per-stock evidence)

This document names the SIGNAL CLUSTERS that travel together across the 61-stock universal-discovery universe. A cluster is a bundle of signals that COVARY — when one fires, others tend to fire — and that names a recognizable mechanism. Clusters are the **patterns the screen should look for**; individual signals are the **inputs to cluster recognition**.

Seven organic clusters + one explicit REJECT cluster were identified.

## CLUSTER 1 — ABANDONED DE-SPAC REANIMATION

### Signal bundle
- De-SPAC at 24-36 months post-merger (Dim 24)
- Sub-$2B at low (Dim 21)
- IGNORED-extreme sentiment (Dim 6)
- Government contract concentration >20% TTM (Dim 3)
- Customer deposit / deferred revenue spike (Dim 13)
- Short interest >15% with rising trajectory (Dim 8)
- Options chain expanded mid-cycle (Dim 18)
- Extended H10 — government bellwether (DoD/NASA/DOE/AFRL/DARPA/Space-Force) (Dim 2)

### Mechanism
SPAC redemption pressure has fully resolved (24+ months past merger), original sponsor lockups expired (typically 12-18 months), PIPE warrants shaken out, chart-bottom is "discoverable" by retail + analysts. The company has had 24+ months to either (a) develop genuine pipeline and government contracts, or (b) fail. Survivors who clear the 24-month mark with growing gov backlog enter a window where the FIRST significant catalyst (gov contract award, NRC certification, design win) triggers the rerate from a deeply-bottomed-out base.

### Universe matches
PL, RKLB, OKLO, SMR, RGTI, QBTS, NNE (recent-IPO functional analog), CTM (probable), USAR, MP, CIFR (Aug 2021 de-SPAC → Sep 2025 Google deal), EOSE (Nov 2020 de-SPAC → mid-2024 Cerberus).

### Hit rate
- Universe-wide: 19/61 = 31%
- DEF/SPACE/NUKE/QC subset: 7-8/13 = 60%
- Critical minerals subset: 3/8 = 38%

### Discrimination test
A de-SPAC at 24-36 months WITHOUT IGNORED sentiment, gov-contract, or chokepoint customer = passive de-SPAC graveyard (no asymmetry). The cluster's signature is the JOINT presence of all 4-6 conditions, not the de-SPAC timing alone.

## CLUSTER 2 — SQUEEZE-AMPLIFIED INFLECTION

### Signal bundle
- Sub-$2B at low (Dim 21)
- IGNORED sentiment (Dim 6)
- Short interest >15% at low with rising direction (Dim 8)
- Extended H10 bellwether mention -3 to -14 months (Dim 2)
- Revenue inflection >40% YoY first-time (Dim 1) OR Customer deposit spike (Dim 13) OR major contract 8-K
- Options chain active (Dim 18)
- Reverse-split-recovery (Dim 19) — biotech/critical-mineral variant

### Mechanism
Stock has to be HATED (IGNORED + sub-$2B cap → low coverage + narrative-neglect) AND structurally short-bait (SI >15% accumulated during the narrative-neglect period) AND have an UNEXPECTED positive catalyst that forces the short side to cover. The catalyst is the bellwether-mention or earnings-inflection; the size of the move is multiplied by the squeeze dynamic. This is the canonical "fundamental + squeeze" pattern that produces 10-100x returns from sub-$500M caps.

### Universe matches
NVTS (28% SI + NVDA partnership), AAOI (24% SI + AVGO 800G + Q3 2024 800G qual), BE (27.6% SI + Meta/Microsoft power-chokepoint + AEP 1GW), AEHR (18-22% SI + NVDA HBM4E), APLD (20%+ SI + NVDA $160M), IREN (15-20% SI + Microsoft $9.7B), BTDR (35% SI + NVDA H100/H200 deploy + 25-35% locked-founder), RKLB (17% SI + SDA $515M), LEU (21% SI + DOE $2.7B), OKLO (16-19% SI + Meta 1.2GW), NNE (21% SI + AFWERX SBIR), QBTS (14-16% SI + Carahsoft + CHIPS LOI), RGTI (15-20% SI + DARPA + AFRL), EOSE (25-30% SI + Cerberus PIPE — but H11 fail).

### Hit rate

- 1 H11-fail false-positive (EOSE) — going-concern + Cerberus rescue produced volatile +1000% peak then -40% — confirms H11 still required even in this cluster

### Cluster vs. RGC-style pure squeeze
RGC has ALL signal weight on Dim 7 (6% float) + Dim 8 (188% borrow) + Dim 19 (38-for-1 FORWARD split) + zero on Dims 1-6. That's a MICROSTRUCTURE PAYOFF, not a chokepoint thesis. Cluster 2 requires Dim 2 (bellwether) + Dim 6 (IGNORED narrative neglect, not promotional pump) + a fundamental catalyst. Without those, the squeeze is RGC-class and belongs in Cluster 8 (REJECT).

## CLUSTER 3 — SPINOFF / REORG CATALYST STRUCTURE

### Signal bundle
- Spinoff or REORG completion -6 to -18 months (Dim 10)
- New CFO or CEO installed at restructure event (Dim 11)
- Options chain CREATED or RESET at restructure (Dim 18)
- First-time risk-factor language in newly-filed 10-K/20-F/6-K (Dim 15)
- Massive new institutional buildout post-event (Dim 17)
- Extended H10 bellwether within 6 months of event (Dim 2)

### Mechanism
A corporate event (spinoff, Ch11 emergence, divestment-driven relisting, major restructuring) creates a FRESH start: cap structure reset, business focus narrowed to a single chokepoint pure-play, institutional float redistributed, analyst coverage re-initiated from scratch, options chain newly listed, risk-factor language entirely re-drafted. When this resets in the 6-18 months BEFORE a chokepoint catalyst, the market rerates from scratch on the new identity — and the magnitude of the rerate is amplified by how thoroughly the old narrative was buried.

### Universe matches
- **Spinoffs:** SNDK (Sandisk spin Feb 2025 → Sep 2025 NAND inflection), INBX (Sanofi spin May 2024 → late-2025 Phase 2 data), WDC (post-Sandisk-spin pure-HDD identity Feb 2025), PVLA (Pieris reverse merger Dec 2024 → Feb 2026 Phase 3)
- **Ch11 emergence:** WOLF (Sep 29 2025 emergence, post-emergence equity +954% YTD), WW (Jun 18 2025 emergence)
- **Divestment-driven relisting:** NBIS (Yandex divest Jul 2024 + name change Aug 2024 + Nasdaq relist Oct 2024 → NVDA PIPE Dec 2024)
- **Refi + new CFO/CEO:** BE (CFO Apr 2024 + refi May 2024 → AEP Nov 2024), LITE (CEO Hurlston Feb 2025 → NVDA $2B Mar 2026)
- **Asset pivot / strategic alternatives:** RLMD (REL-1017 fail Dec 2024 → NDV-01 in-license Mar 2025 → Apr 2025 Phase 2 data), TOI (Mar 2025 debt restructure + $16.5M PIPE → first profitable Adj EBITDA Q4 2025)
- **De-SPAC + new CEO at event:** USAR (de-SPAC Mar 2025 + Joshua Ballard CEO)

### Hit rate
- Universe-wide: 22/61 = 36%
- Cross-sector — strongest in biotech (45%) and AI-infra optical-memory (33%)

### Critical insight
**CFO/CEO change at the restructure event is the under-rated leading governance signal.** 4/12 in AI-optical batch alone; 20% universe-wide; high quality when present. New CFOs install capital-discipline and message the chokepoint story differently than incumbent management would.

## CLUSTER 4 — GOV CONTRACT CONCENTRATION ANCHOR

### Signal bundle
- Government contract >20% of TTM revenue (Dim 3) — and ideally >50%
- Customer deposit / deferred revenue spike from gov progress payments (Dim 13)
- Extended H10 bellwether = DoD / NASA / DOE / NRO / Space-Force / DARPA / AFRL / SDA / EXIM (Dim 2)
- Sub-$5B (Dim 21)
- IGNORED-extreme to IGNORED sentiment pre-award (Dim 6)
- Multi-year contract award structure ($100M+ IDIQ or $50M+ definitive award)

### Mechanism
For defense/space/nuclear/quantum/critical-minerals chokepoints, government IS the bellwether. A major gov contract concentration (>20% TTM revenue) signals (a) the company is positioned at a real chokepoint the government has identified as strategic, (b) the bilateral contract structure typically includes prepayments / progress billings visible in deferred revenue, (c) defense/space contracts run multi-year and cannot be easily reassigned — durable revenue visibility, and (d) the gov-anointing IS the chokepoint validation.

## CLUSTER 5 — BROKEN-IPO / POST-FAILURE PIVOT

### Signal bundle
- IGNORED-extreme sentiment driven by prior failure (Phase 3 fail / IPO disappointment / sector collapse) (Dim 6)
- Sub-$500M at low; often sub-$100M (Dim 21)
- Reverse split OR avoided-reverse-split via catalyst (Dim 19)
- Sub-$1 to sub-$5 stock geometry at low
- Asset pivot, restructuring, or in-license event -6 to -12 months (Dim 10)
- New CEO/CFO -6 to -18 months (Dim 11) — sometimes
- Specialist healthcare-fund (BVF, Frazier, Deerfield) PIPE participation OR insider PIPE participation (Dim 4)
- Catalyst that surprises a fully-extinguished bull case

### Mechanism
A prior bull thesis was crushed (Phase 3 trial failure, broken IPO, narrative collapse). The company's float is small, the stock is sub-$1 or sub-$5, retail has abandoned, sell-side coverage is minimal. Management pivots — in-licenses a new asset, raises a structured PIPE, restructures debt, swaps in new leadership. A SECOND catalyst (Phase 2 on new asset, FDA designation, commercial inflection) surprises the market into the dollar-stock geometry, which then offers leveraged returns simply due to the small-dollar-base.

### Universe matches
- **Biotech:** RLMD (REL-1017 fail → NDV-01 pivot via Trigone in-license March 2025 → Apr 2025 Phase 2 92% ORR), CRDO (broken-IPO post-guide-down at $7.20 → Q1 FY24 hyperscaler ramp), PVLA (reverse merger w/ Pieris shell Dec 2024 → Feb 2026 Phase 3 SELVA met), SLS (chronic dilution → REGAL Phase 3 interim + SLS009 Phase 2a + Fast Track convergence), SLGL (10-for-1 reverse split May 2025 + Twyneo/Epsolay commercial inflection), TOI (March 2025 debt restructure + Helios partnership → first profitable Adj EBITDA Q4 2025)
- **AI-infra:** POET (broken-micro-cap $5.97 52-wk avg → Lumilens 6-K May 2026), NVTS (broken-IPO declining sub-$2 → NVDA partnership May 2025), AXTI (NASDAQ delisting notice Jul 2025 → CPO rotation)

### Hit rate
- Universe-wide: 12-15/61 = 20-25%
- Biotech: 5-6/11 = 45-55%
- AI-infra micro-cap: 3-4/24 = 12-17%

### Critical insight
**Sub-$1 dollar-stock geometry is a force multiplier.** A $0.50 → $5 move is +1000%; a $50 → $500 move is also +1000% but requires 10x more dollar flow. The mechanical math of small-dollar-base recovery is structurally easier when sentiment correctly resets.

## CLUSTER 6 — COMMODITY MACRO BETA (CORRECTLY FILTERED OUT BY FRAMEWORK)

### Signal bundle
- Revenue inflection driven by commodity-price (Dim 1)
- Already-held Vanguard/BlackRock/State Street (Dim 17)
- Mature sell-side coverage (no initiation lead) (Dim 5)
- $2B+ market cap (often borderline H8) (Dim 21)
- NEUTRAL-to-PARTIAL sentiment (Dim 6)
- No bellwether mention (Dim 2) — pure macro thesis
- No customer deposit spike (Dim 13) — spot-priced commodities

### Mechanism
Pure macro beta on the underlying commodity (silver, gold, copper, oil-services). Operating leverage on rising commodity price produces a fundamental revenue inflection. But NO chokepoint-specific tell, no bellwether anointing, no asymmetric setup — institutional ownership is already broad, coverage is mature, sentiment is already PARTIAL. Returns top out in the 100-300% range, not 500%+ except in extreme commodity moves.

### Universe matches
ASM (Avino Silver, 39% YoY rev), CDE (Coeur Mining, SilverCrest acquisition + commodity), HL (Hecla, pure silver-price proxy), PAAS (Pan American Silver, 119% YoY), FET (Forum Energy + Variperm M&A)

### Hit rate
- Universe-wide: 5-7/61 = 8-12%
- These names APPEAR in the universe at 200-300% returns (closer to the 500% threshold) but rarely cross 500% — confirming the framework's specificity is correct to NOT prioritize them

### Critical insight
**This cluster is what NOT to pursue.** Operating commodity-producer beta is real but lower-magnitude. The framework's H10 chokepoint-bellwether requirement correctly filters these out, even when revenue inflection (Dim 1) fires. The right approach: monitor commodity-producer revenue inflection as a market-color signal, NOT a candidate signal.

### Forward screen
N/A — this cluster is EXPLICITLY NOT screened for. Documented to avoid framework drift into commodity-beta territory.

## CLUSTER 7 — LEGACY-INDUSTRIAL-CAPACITY-PIVOT (REORG variant, accepted blind spot)

### Signal bundle
- Legacy operating business with REPURPOSABLE manufacturing capacity (boilers, turbines, generators, transformers, fab tools)
- Going-concern qualification or near-bankruptcy distress (Dim 15) — FAILS H11
- Penny-stock geometry (sub-$1)
- Anchor-customer LOI / LNTP signs the entity into a new chokepoint role
- IGNORED-extreme sentiment from "broken industrial" narrative

### Mechanism
A legacy industrial (often coal-utility-boiler, gas-turbine, transformer, or fab-tool) with manufacturing capacity that turns out to be REPURPOSABLE to AI-data-center power once a hyperscaler validates the pathway. The asset (manufacturing footprint) is repurposable but not pre-identified ex ante. The leading signal — the anchor-customer LNTP — IS the inflection. The framework cannot screen this ex ante because H8 requires revenue today and H11 fails on going-concern.

### Universe matches
BW (Babcock & Wilcox, $0.45-0.62 low → +30-49x — Applied Digital LNTP Nov 2025 IS the inflection)

### Hit rate
- Universe-wide: 1/61 = 2%
- Documented as ACCEPTED BLIND SPOT in Archos research

### Critical insight
**This is a TRUE blind spot, accepted as the price of strictness.** Admitting BW would mean loosening H11 to admit going-concern legacy industrials → false-positive risk explodes. The pattern produces wins occasionally but the precision degradation is too costly.

### Forward screen
**No.** Documented for awareness but not screened. The pattern variant exists; the framework correctly accepts the miss to preserve specificity.

## CLUSTER 8 — MEME / SQUEEZE STRUCTURAL ANOMALY (REJECT)

### Signal bundle
- Free float <20% (Dim 7) — often via 80%+ CEO/founder ownership
- Annualized borrow fee >50% — often >100% or >150% (Dim 8 extreme variant)
- FORWARD stock split (e.g., 38-for-1) to bring retail access — OPPOSITE direction from Dim 19 reverse-split recovery
- ZERO bellwether mention (Dim 2)
- ZERO government contract (Dim 3)
- Minimal or zero revenue (Dim 1)
- ZERO insider buying cluster (Dim 4) — CEO already controls 80%+
- ZERO sell-side coverage
- Foreign issuer (Dim 23) — typically Cayman/HK/Asia domicile
- Paid-promotion footprint (RedChip-style) OR retail FOMO catalyst (Reddit/X)

### Mechanism
Pure structural microstructure payoff. CEO concentration locks supply; retail FOMO meets bottled-up short interest; forward stock split converts an institutional-only $877 stock into a $23 retail toy. The move has nothing to do with a chokepoint, a bellwether, or a fundamental catalyst. The +82,000% return is mathematical squeeze geometry, not investable thesis.

### Universe matches
**RGC** (Regencell Bioscience — 6% float, 188% borrow, 38-for-1 forward split, +82,000% peak)
**SHAZ** (SharonAI — Stage 1 framework PASSED via NVIDIA NCP cert; Stage 2 DD REJECTED 6/6 RED on counterparty insolvency + DeFi lender + paid-promo + management self-dealing)
**Possibly NINE** (low-quality fundamental rally on short-cover + commodity sentiment, but revenue declining; not pure squeeze)

### Hit rate
- Universe-wide: 2-3/61 = 3-5%

### Critical insight
**This cluster is EXPLICITLY a REJECT pattern.** The microstructure payoff is real but uninvestable — it requires perfect entry timing into a vehicle with no fundamental anchor; the chance of being on the right side of the squeeze is structurally low, and the asymmetry on the wrong side (being a forced seller during the unwind) is catastrophic. Framework should EXPLICITLY screen these OUT before any 6-check evaluation.
