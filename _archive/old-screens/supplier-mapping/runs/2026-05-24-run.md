## Methodology note

EdgarTools `search_filings_full_text` boolean AND syntax with phrase
quotes returned zero results (same issue as revenue-inflection screen).
Switched to single-name phrase queries and triaged the snippets.

The disambiguation step (filing-context read) is the most expensive
step. Most "NVIDIA" or "Microsoft" mentions in 10-Ks are:
- Competitor lists ("we compete with Microsoft Teams")
- Software dependency disclosures ("we rely on Microsoft licenses")
- Personnel bios ("prior to joining, served at Microsoft")
- Supplier disclosures (the company sources GPUs FROM NVIDIA, not sells TO)

True customer-concentration disclosures (the target signal) appear in:
- Item 1 Business — "X represented N% of revenue"
- Risk Factors — "loss of key customer X would materially affect..."
- XBRL CustomerConcentrationRisk tags

The most actionable hits this run were customer-concentration
disclosures with named bellwethers. Highest-conviction hit: **Fabrinet
(FN) — NVIDIA 27.6% of revenue FY2025; Cisco 18.2%**. But FN is now
$27.3B mkt cap and excluded on H8.

## Top names (narrative)

### 1. FN (Fabrinet) — the cleanest one-degree NVIDIA supplier disclosure

Filing passage (verbatim, FY2025 10-K, filing date 2025-08-19):
> "During fiscal years 2025 and 2024, we had two customers that each
> contributed 10% or more of our revenues. During fiscal year 2025,
> NVIDIA Corporation and Cisco Systems, Inc. contributed 27.6% and
> 18.2%, respectively, of our revenues."

What they supply: advanced optical packaging, transceivers, tunable
lasers, active optical cables, custom optics. Fabrinet is the
contract-manufacturer-of-choice for the optical-transceiver vendors
that supply NVIDIA's networking layer. The 27.6% NVDA concentration
went from ~0% in 2022 to 27.6% in 2025 — a textbook chokepoint pull-
through.

Chokepoint mapping: #8 (optical interconnect). FN's chokepoint
exposure is real and large.

**Why excluded:** $27.3B mkt cap is 5.4× the H8 ceiling. FN was
sub-$5B circa 2022 ($30-50/share); now at $703. The framework
would have caught FN three years ago if the screen had existed.
The fact that the screen *would* have caught FN at the inflection
point validates the screen design — but the current run is too
late for the current rerate.

**Action:** add FN to the "framework-confirmed retroactive winner"
log. Use FN's pre-rerate H10/H8/H5/H11 signature as a calibration
case for the supplier-mapping screen.

## Refinement notes

### Query effectiveness per bellwether
- **NVIDIA:** 34 hits, ~30% disambiguation cost. Best signal-to-noise of the bellwethers.
- **Taiwan Semiconductor:** 36 hits, mostly XBRL-tagged customer concentration disclosures. Caught Qnity, FormFactor — both interesting but mega-cap.
- **Microsoft:** 64 hits, ~5% actionable. Mostly competitor / software-dependency / personnel-bio noise. Need a narrower query (e.g. "Microsoft" NEAR "customer" + "revenue").
- **Meta Platforms:** 47 hits, ~5% actionable. Mostly competitor lists. The Meta-as-customer pattern is rare and tends to surface via large-cap REITs/utilities or pre-revenue power deals.

### Disambiguation cost
- ~75% of raw hits across all bellwethers are non-customer mentions.
- ~20% are customer mentions but the company is mega-cap (excluded on H8)
- ~5% are actionable
- This was the budgeted ratio per the screen design

### The AXTI test result (mandatory validation)
**AXTI's recent 10-Ks (2025, 2024, 2023, 2022) do NOT name any bellwether by name.** The only EdgarTools matches for AXTI + (NVIDIA/Lumentum/Coherent/Broadcom) are from 2003-2006 — too old to be relevant.

This **confirms the documented limitation of the v1 screen**: it
catches one-degree suppliers (companies that name a bellwether
directly) but **misses two-degree suppliers** (companies that supply
the suppliers — AXTI sells InP wafers to Lumentum/Coherent/EMCORE,
who then sell to NVIDIA).

The AXTI test fails as expected. The v2 extension to two-degree
mapping is genuinely needed and explicitly flagged.

### Tooling friction
- **Boolean AND with phrase quotes returns 0 hits** in EdgarTools — same issue as revenue-inflection screen. Workaround: single-phrase queries + manual triage.
- **WebFetch returns 403 on SEC.gov URLs** — can't fetch 10-K content for direct read.
- **`filing_section` with `item_1` errors** — must use `business_overview` instead. Documented for next run.
- **`filing_section` accession lookups 404'd on two AAOI/AXTI accessions** — possibly stale CIK paths. Need to validate accession formatting.

### What to change for next run
1. **v2 two-degree extension**: when surfacing a one-degree supplier (e.g. FN, AAOI), also map their named upstream suppliers and search those. Example: AAOI's 10-K likely names its substrate supplier — search that supplier in next run.
2. **Add AVGO and AMD queries** (not run this session due to time budget).
3. **Add 20-F query** for foreign-listed suppliers (Sakura Internet pattern). Try once with bellwether queries.
4. **Narrow Microsoft / Meta queries** to use NEAR proximity ("Microsoft" NEAR "customer", 10) once boolean syntax is confirmed.
5. **Build the two-degree retroactive validation matrix** — confirm that the upstream layers of AAOI, CRDO, NVTS each name their downstream bellwether customer (one-degree from us = bellwether; two-degree from us = the AXTI/POET layer).
