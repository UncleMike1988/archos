### SANM — v1 reading corrected: segment growth is from M&A acquisition

SANM's Communication Networks Cloud & AI Infrastructure segment Q2
FY26: **$2.77B, +280% YoY** ([SANM Q2 FY2026 10-Q, filed 2026-04-27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/897723/000089772326000025/0000897723-26-000025-index.htm)).

Decomposition:
- Core Sanmina: $891M, +22% YoY (organic)
- **ZT Systems**: $1.88B, "driven entirely by AMD-based accelerated compute"

ZT Systems was acquired from AMD in **October 2024** for $3B in a deal
where AMD retained the GPU design business and divested the systems
business to Sanmina. The Q2 FY26 segment data is therefore comparing:
- This year: SANM + ZT Systems (combined entity)
- Last year: SANM only

So the "3.78x YoY growth" is **mechanically an acquisition effect**,
not an organic inflection. Excluding ZT Systems, the segment grew
22% YoY, which fails the >40% threshold.

This is the **M&A pivot blind spot** documented in INSIGHTS.md
(SEI pattern): "acquisition acquires the chokepoint exposure. Pre-pivot
identity unrelated to AI. Catch via M&A news flow, not via 4-filter
test."

The framework correctly rejects SANM on this basis:
- H8 fail: mkt cap $13.8B AND organic AI-DC growth doesn't dominate
- M&A pivot: the "transition" was a corporate transaction, not a
  demand-driven inflection
- The market has already re-rated SANM in response to the ZT deal

### PENG — v1 reading was wrong: segment is DECLINING, not growing

V1 reported PENG had "AI segment growing inside a stalling company."
The Q2 FY2026 segment data ([from earnings call, 2026-04-01](https://ir.penguinsolutions.com/news/news-details/2026/Penguin-Solutions-Reports-Q2-Fiscal-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx)) shows the opposite:

- Advanced Computing (HPC/AI) segment: $116M Q2 FY26, **DOWN 42% YoY**
- 34% of total revenue (down from 50%+ in prior year)
- Driver: wind-down of Penguin Edge + reduced hyperscale hardware sales
- Forward bookings strong, but revenue contracted in the quarter

PENG announced an AMD partnership and raised forward outlook; stock
rallied 13%. But the segment-level NUMBER is contracting in the
current quarter. **PENG fails Level 2 on the segment data.** The
framework correctly does not flag.

### Transition ETA calculation

For Level 2 to fire, a $5-15B mid-cap needs a segment that is:
(a) AI/data-center-related per the company's disclosure,
(b) >40% YoY growth (organic, not M&A),
(c) <50% of total today (so transition is forward-looking, not already
    priced),
(d) >15% of total today (so transition timeline is plausible <18 months).

Neither SANM (already transitioned + M&A driven) nor PENG (declining)
satisfies the test. **Zero Level 2 strict passes this run.**

### BTDR — Bitdeer Technologies (NEW Level 3 surface, separate watch queue)

**Profile** (from [BTDR FY2025 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001899123&type=6-K&dateb=&owner=include&count=40) filings and company brief):
- Cayman-Islands-incorporated, Nasdaq-listed (BTDR)
- Files 6-K not 10-Q (foreign private issuer)
- FY2025 net income: $65.6M
- Total assets: $2.8B
- 3 business segments: self-mining, hash rate sharing/hosting, **AI/HPC cloud (developing)**
- CEO Jihan Wu (legendary BTC figure, ex-Bitmain founder)
- DC ops in US, Norway, Bhutan
- AI Cloud Q1 2026 ARR: **$43M, +105% month-over-month growth**

**Why this is a Level 3 (not Level 2) candidate:**

The AI segment is <10% of total revenue today. The framework's Level 2
threshold requires the segment to be already 15%+ of revenue. BTDR's AI
segment is below that threshold but is growing at a rate that would
push it past the threshold within 6 months if the +105% MoM rate holds
even partially.

**Why it goes to a SEPARATE watch queue (not framework ACCEPT):**

Per INSIGHTS.md: sector-pivot blind-spot pattern is explicitly
out-of-scope for the four-filter framework. "Pre-pivot identity
unrelated to AI. Catch via M&A news flow / sector-pivot tracking,
not via 4-filter test."

BTDR is the BTC-mining-pivot pattern at an EARLIER stage than IREN
(which is already in Tier 2 DD with $9.7B MSFT + $3.4B NVDA contracts).
IREN is the matured version; BTDR is at the pre-validation stage.

**The framework correctly does not directly ACCEPT BTDR** because:
- No bellwether name-drop (H10 vendor-level absent; only category-level
  AI-cloud is implied)
- Revenue mix today is not AI-dominant (H8 product-line is BTC mining
  + hash rate services)
- Foreign-filer handicap (no 10-K, no Form 4, no US 13F surfacing)

**Recommendation:** add BTDR to a SEPARATE "BTC-pivot watch list" — the
analog to the existing CANDIDATE_UNIVERSE Tier 2 watch for IREN and
CLSK. The watch is to monitor:
- Does the AI ARR keep compounding? (next 2 quarters)
- Does BTDR file an 8-K / 6-K announcing a hyperscaler or NVDA partnership?
- Does the AI segment cross 25-30% of total revenue?

If yes to any, this becomes a candidate for the same Tier 2 process
that surfaced IREN and CLSK.

**Risk per INSIGHTS.md:** "CoinShares Q1 2026 report: publicly listed
Bitcoin miners could derive up to 70% of revenues from AI by Dec 2026,
up from ~30% today." This is industry-level macro; doesn't validate
any single name. The pattern that works (IREN, CLSK) is concentrated
at specific names with executed hyperscaler contracts. BTDR is
pre-contract — that's the entry-window risk.
