- Confidence shorthand: **[H]** = high confidence (multiple sources); **[M]** = medium (single source); **[L]** = low (inferred/domain knowledge only); **[N/A]** = not pulled.

## Cross-batch patterns observed

### Pattern 1: HIGH SHORT INTEREST as fuel for the violent re-rate (NEW SIGNAL — not previously isolated)
The strongest unorthodox signal across this batch is elevated pre-move short interest acting as the **violent-re-rate fuel** that turns a fundamental inflection into a multi-bagger. **NVTS (~28% SI), AAOI (24% SI), BE (27.6% SI), AEHR (18-22% SI), APLD (20%+ SI), IREN (15-20% SI)** all entered their inflection windows with elevated to extreme short interest. The pattern is consistent: a stock has to be HATED (H5 IGNORED sentiment) AND structurally short-bait (high SI%) AND have an unexpected positive catalyst (H10 chokepoint mention). The first two conditions are partially redundant — H5 sentiment IS often what creates the short interest — but the elevated SI% is the *mechanism* that converts the catalyst into the 10-100x return. Recommend adding **"SI > 15% in 3 months before inflection"** as a new candidate signal H12 to test in future runs.

### Pattern 2: SPINOFF / RESTRUCTURING / RELISTING as a 6-12 month leading event (NEW SIGNAL)
A surprisingly consistent pattern: **SNDK (spinoff Feb 2025 → inflection Sep 2025, ~6mo lead)**, **NBIS (Yandex divestment Jul 2024, name change Aug 2024, Nasdaq relisting Oct 2024 → inflection Dec 2024, ~6 weeks lead)**, **BE (May 2024 refi + Apr 2024 CFO replacement → inflection Nov 2024, ~6-7mo lead)**, **APLD (BTC→AI strategic pivot 2023-24 → inflection Sep 2024)**, **AAOI (convertible refinancing cycle 2024 → inflection Nov 2024)**. The mechanism: corporate restructuring forces a fresh institutional float / new analyst coverage / new narrative, and combined with a chokepoint catalyst, the market rerates from scratch. Half the batch had a *material* structural reorganization in the 12 months before inflection. This deserves explicit testing as **H13: corporate restructuring (spinoff / refi / relisting / CFO replacement) in trailing 12 months acts as a setup amplifier.**

### Pattern 3: NEW CFO / FINANCIAL LEADERSHIP REPLACEMENT as a leading hint (UNDER-RATED)
**BE installed a new CFO (Berenbaum) ~6.5 months before the AEP 1GW inflection — and the broader unorthodox signal stack predicted the rerate would crystallize as the new CFO communicated.** This is a single data point but worth tracking systematically. New CFOs often raise capital pre-narrative-shift (BE refi'd in May 2024 with the new CFO), and pre-narrative-shift balance sheet repair is what allows the eventual rerate to sustain. **Recommend pulling CFO/CEO replacement dates across all framework-passing candidates in future screens.**

### Pattern 4: SHELF REGISTRATION USED CONCURRENT, NOT BEFORE (CONTRARY TO HYPOTHESIS)
The original hypothesis that "shelf registration filed but not raised" was a leading signal **does not hold cleanly in this batch**. The pattern is the inverse: shelves were filed AHEAD but USED at or shortly AFTER inflection (NBIS $2B NVDA Dec 2024 = the catalyst; IREN $2.3B convert Dec 2025 = post-Microsoft; AAOI Dec 2024 convertible exchange = post-Q3; POET $400M direct = post-Lumilens; APLD NVDA PIPE = at inflection). Shelves are pre-positioned capacity but the *use* is concurrent with the rerate. **Recommend dropping "shelf without use" as a leading signal and replacing it with "ATM/shelf USED to monetize the inflection within 0 to +90 days" as a CONFIRMATION pattern.**
