## CONTEXT

NOVT was surfaced by the Physical AI / Robotics Scan (2026-05-28) as a TIER 4 SEGMENT candidate. The thesis is that Novanta's ATI Industrial Automation segment (force/torque sensors for robotics) is the segment-level VPG analog — growing faster than the parent, exposed to humanoid robotics demand, but buried inside a $5.5B diversified parent that the market values at a blended industrial multiple.

Profile summary:
- Novanta Inc. (Nasdaq: NOVT)
- Market cap: ~$5.45-5.82B
- Business: Diversified precision technology — photonics, vision, precision motion, and automation solutions. Three segments: Precision Medicine & Manufacturing, Robotics & Automation, and ATI Industrial Automation (acquired 2021).
- H8 tier: TIER 4 SEGMENT ($5-15B parent with qualifying AI/robotics segment)
- ATI Industrial Automation: world's leading force/torque sensor manufacturer for industrial and collaborative robots. Recently expanding into humanoid robotics with 10+ OEM engagements and NVIDIA Halos Lab integration.
- The TIER 4 thesis: the market prices NOVT at a blended industrial multiple (~20-25x P/E). If the ATI segment is growing 40%+ on humanoid demand while the parent grows 10-15%, the segment is undervalued. When humanoid revenue becomes material, the market re-rates the whole company on the robotics multiple.

Why NOVT matters:
ATI Industrial Automation is the gold standard for force/torque sensors in robotics. Every major cobot manufacturer (Universal Robots, Fanuc, ABB, KUKA) uses ATI sensors. If humanoid robots need the same sensing (and they do — every joint and gripper needs force feedback), ATI is the incumbent supplier with the broadest installed base and the most OEM relationships. This is the POWL of robotics sensing — the component everyone needs, made by the company everyone already works with.
