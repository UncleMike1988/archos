## CONTEXT

ALNT was surfaced by the Physical AI / Robotics Scan (2026-05-28) as the strongest "next VPG" pattern match — a precision motion systems company actively self-positioning into the humanoid robotics chokepoint.

Profile summary:
- Allient Inc. (Nasdaq: ALNT)
- Market cap: ~$1.08B
- Business: Precision motion systems — motors, drives, controllers, gearboxes for industrial automation, medical, defense, and robotics applications. Vertically integrated motion platform.
- H8 tier: TIER 2 CATALYST ($500M-$2B)
- Robotics positioning: Published 3 whitepapers/webinars in 33 days (April-May 2026) on thermally-optimized humanoid joints. Presented at Robotics Summit 2026. This is active supplier self-positioning — the company is telling the market "we make what humanoid robots need."
- H10-R status: FLAG — no binding humanoid OEM customer named yet. Self-positioning is not the same as a supply agreement.
- Proposed Chokepoint #15: Humanoid Motor-Control & Sensor-Fusion Silicon (multi-candidate, fragmented)
- The VPG parallel: VPG was a boring precision measurement company that rerated 5x when the market connected "force sensors" to "humanoid robots." ALNT makes the MOTORS and DRIVES that move the joints — a more fundamental component than sensors.

Why ALNT matters:
Every humanoid robot joint needs a motor + drive + gearbox to move. ALNT makes integrated motion systems combining all three. If Tesla Optimus, Figure, or Aptronic selects ALNT's motion platform for production humanoids, the revenue step-function would be enormous — thousands of motors per robot × millions of robots = a volume ramp that dwarfs ALNT's current industrial base.

The risk: ALNT has zero named humanoid customers. The whitepapers and conference presentations are marketing, not contracts. Japanese incumbents (Harmonic Drive, Nabtesco, Yaskawa) dominate the precision motion market. ALNT may be positioning into a chokepoint it can't actually win.
