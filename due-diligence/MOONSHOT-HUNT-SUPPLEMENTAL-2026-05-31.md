### Required deliverable — ASYS reflow vs. hybrid bonding: runway EXTENDS, not bypassed

**Verdict: HBM4 does not bypass ASYS's reflow runway.** The hybrid-bonding transition hits a
*different* process step than where reflow ovens live, and it arrives a generation later than base
HBM4. Precise process geography:

- **Where ASYS's BTU mass-reflow ovens are used — and stay used through HBM4:** the CoWoS/2.5D flow
  uses solder mass-reflow at **two package-level stages**, both untouched by hybrid bonding —
  **(1) Chip-on-Wafer:** the logic die (GPU) *and* the pre-built HBM stacks are attached onto the
  silicon interposer via **flip-chip mass-reflow** ("the wafer with all die placed is baked in a
  reflow oven"); **(2) Chip-on-Substrate:** the interposer assembly is solder-attached to the organic
  package substrate, then BGA ball-attach to the board. These remain solder-reflow-based for HBM4 and
  the foreseeable GPU generations.
- **Where hybrid bonding actually attacks:** the **DRAM-to-DRAM stacking *inside* the HBM cube** —
  today done with **TCB / MR-MUF** (a different tool class from ASYS's conveyor reflow ovens anyway).
  Roadmap: **Samsung** targets hybrid bonding for HBM4 (samples 2025) but production only from
  **HBM4E 16-high**; **SK Hynix** sticks with **MR-MUF for HBM4**, hybrid bonding as backup, from
  **HBM4E (~20-high)**. Consensus: **hybrid bonding inside the cube ramps at HBM4E (2027+), not base
  HBM4 (2026)**, gradually and dual-tracked, and it replaces **TCB tools, not package-level reflow.**

**Net:** ASYS's reflow position is anchored at the **GPU-package level** (chip-on-wafer-on-substrate
attach, BGA), which the hybrid-bonding shift does not touch; disruption risk from hybrid bonding is
**low through HBM4 and indirect even at HBM4E.** The genuine ASYS flaws stay the ones already in the
file (reflow is competitive vs Heller/Rehm/Kurtz Ersa; total revenue at trough; AI is ~20% of total)
— **the hybrid-bonding "bypass" is not a real additional kill-switch.** This is a net positive for the
existing thesis.

**Unchanged:** ASYS's own known flaws are intact and still the real DD (total revenue at a ~$79M trough;

## Bottom line
**ASYS holds as #1.** Serenity's three open themes are real and large, but their sub-$500M US-listed
