### #1: ENOV — Enovis Corp (NYSE)
**The literal "ADBE playbook at smaller scale." Conviction: HIGHEST as a
risk-adjusted de-rate — honest shape ~1.5–2.5x, with a 3x available on full
multiple normalization. EQUITY-ONLY (no LEAPS).**

**The numbers (5/29).** Price **$22.68**, ~57.6M shares → **~$1.31B cap**, net
debt ~$1.27B → **EV ~$2.57B.** Q1-2026 (ended 4/3/26) revenue **$589M, +5%

**TTM rev ~$2.25–2.3B.** FY2026 guide reaffirmed: **$2.31–2.37B rev, $425–435M
adj EBITDA, adj EPS $3.52–3.73.** → **~0.57x EV/sales, ~6x EV/adj-EBITDA** vs an
ortho/med-device peer set at 8–12x. Q1 adj EBITDA $104M (17.6% mgn); Q1 GAAP net
loss only $(8)M.

**The catalyst that drives the 3x — and why it's not priced.** The FY2025 GAAP
loss of **$1.18B was a $1.05B NON-CASH goodwill impairment** mechanically
triggered by the share-price-vs-carrying-value test (the market re-rating *forced*
the writedown; nothing was discovered to be impaired operationally). That loss
makes ENOV screen radioactive to every GAAP-net-income-screened institution — so
it sits orphaned at ~6x EBITDA. As the excess goodwill finishes burning off and
the income statement normalizes, those buyers can re-engage. The covenant (senior
secured leverage ≤3.50x; running ~3.1x; recently amended to let $400M cash offset
debt, maturities pushed to 2030) means there is **no forced-seller / dilution
risk** while you wait. The engine — Recon (Nebula hip, ARG shoulder, extremities
+10% US) — is genuinely accelerating.

**Bull / base / bear.**
- **Bull (~$60–68, ~2.7–3x):** Recon holds ~8–10% organic, FCF inflects as the
  instrument-set capex wave normalizes, GAAP turns clean → market pays ~11–12x the
  ~$435M adj EBITDA (EV ~$5–5.2B, cap ~$3.9B). The full multiple-normalization 3x.
- **Base (~$34–40, ~1.5–1.8x):** guide lands, multiple re-rates from ~6x toward
  ~8–9x EBITDA; tracks the *cut* analyst targets ($36–37) plus a bit. The likely case.
- **Bear (~$15–17, −30%):** capex stays ~$197M so FCF stays ~$20M, the market
  decides 6x EBITDA is *correct* for a ~3–5% blended grower with a dead P&R half
  → value trap, no re-rate.

**What kills it.** Free cash flow. FY2025: $217M operating cash flow − **$197M
capex = $20M FCF** on a ~$2.6B EV. If the Recon instrument-set capex doesn't ease,
"cheap on EBITDA" is a mirage and the de-rate was the market being *right*. The
flat P&R segment (half the company) is the second drag — blended organic is only
~3% because Recon's 6–8% is diluted by a dead-flat legacy half.

**How the market classifies it (and why that's wrong).** Tagged a "money-losing,
levered, no-growth medtech roll-up that overpaid for Lima." Reality: the GAAP loss
is non-cash accounting, leverage has real headroom and declining maturities, and
the part that matters (Recon) is *gaining share and accelerating*. The market is
pricing the accounting, not the business.

**LEAPS check.** **NONE.** ENOV's longest-dated listed calls expire **Jan-15-2027**
(~7.5 months) — there is **no Jan-2028 LEAPS**, strikes are sparse ($12.50–$35),
and the chain is thin (small-cap medtech). The "pair with an affordable LEAPS" leg
of the playbook is unavailable — **this is an equity expression, full stop.** (Live
IV not pullable; moot given no 18-month tenor.)

**2x leveraged single-stock ETF.** None — confirmed by absence across Direxion /
GraniteShares / T-Rex / Tradr, whose single-stock lineups are mega-cap-only. Not
plausible at this size/options-liquidity.

**WINNER_UNIVERSE analog.** The **de-rated-quality / Bucket-3 ADBE-at-smaller-scale**
shape — a real, profitable franchise sold off on a fear (accounting optics) that
isn't operational deterioration. Lower torque than a moonshot, but the cleanest,

**Bull / base / bear (equity, before LEAPS leverage).**

~$105 intrinsic, a ~2.5–3x on an ATM call. The high IV means you overpay at entry
and bleed theta if the second leg is slow — size accordingly. **This is the holy
grail the brief described: a 1.5–2x equity becomes 3–5x on a structured LEAPS.**

**2x leveraged single-stock ETF.** None found (no INODD/INODU). Leverage must come

## What the hunt revealed about the $1-5B landscape

1. **"Already crossed $1B" becomes "already ran 130–230%."** The single most
   recurring failure mode: the name that screened as a clean inflection had already
   priced it (INOD +130%, XMTR +162%, AMBQ ~+230%). At $1-5B you are usually buying
   *after* the proof, so the discipline is "is there a *second* leg and am I paying a
   sane price for it" — exactly the CLAUDE.md question — and the answer was "rich" as
   often as "yes."

2. **The adversarial pass earned its keep — it flipped three of four lead theses.**
   XMTR's cap was understated 2x and it's lossmaking with net debt; TARS's
   profitability is 2027 not 2026; SHLS's entire "AI-chokepoint" framing is ~0.7% of
   revenue with the company having *self-reclassified its SIC code to "Semiconductors"
   ahead of the revenue.* Leading with the bull case (per the bird-dog mandate) is
   right — but the hard, unflinching second look is what kept three narratives out of
   the Final Five on top.

3. **Fraud-gate density is high here too, just dressed better.** Sub-$1B promos are
   crude (paid IR, shells); $1-5B "fraud" is litigation- and accounting-shaped — DLO
   (Argentina fabricated-transactions probe), SEI (convicted-felon turbine
   allegation), SOUN/GLOB/WGS (active securities suits), DCO (restatement). The gate
   did real work; several otherwise-attractive names failed it.

4. **The LEAPS holy grail is rarer than the brief hoped.** Of eight verified
   finalists, only **INOD and VRNS** have a *usable* Jan-2028 chain — and INOD's is
   high-IV. ENOV and AMBQ (two of the best fundamental setups) have **no LEAPS at
   all**; XMTR and ALKT top out at Jan-2027/Dec-2026. **No 2x single-stock ETF exists
   for any of these** — the providers cover mega-caps only. The "3x equity → 5–7x
   LEAPS" structure is available on INOD and VRNS; everywhere else it's equity-only.

5. **Honest answer to the prompt:** there is no name in this verified set where a
   *clean, fundamentally-supported, today-priced* 12-month 3x is sitting unpriced.
   **ENOV is the best risk-adjusted shot** (cheapest, most-defensible, catalyst not
   priced — but equity-only and FCF-gated). **INOD is the best holy-grail vehicle**
   (real engine + cleared cloud + a LEAPS that turns a 1.5–2x into 3–5x). **AMBQ is
   the literal-3x torque** if you want to swing for the fence and accept you can't
   lever it. That is what I'd put in front of you, with honest return shapes, rather
   than overstate a 3x the data won't carry.
