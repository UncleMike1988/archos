**Shape:** roughly symmetric, skewed by *which regime wins* — operating leverage on a real automotive

## 4. The photonixFAB / NVIDIA silicon-photonics thesis (the brief's MOST IMPORTANT leg) — REAL but as an R&D evaluation seat, not a hidden production relationship.

This is the load-bearing claim, and the honest verdict is: **the kernel is true and *publicly
documented*; the leap to "hidden NVIDIA high-volume manufacturing" is unsupported and contradicted
by NVIDIA's own production supply chain.**

**What photonixFAB actually is.** Not an X-FAB-owned silicon-photonics production line — it is an
**EU-funded ~€47.6M R&D consortium and shared-access prototyping platform** (Chips JU / KDT JU +
national co-funding), **led/coordinated by X-FAB**, running on X-FAB's **200mm line at
Corbeil-Essonnes, France**, hosting imec's SOI platform plus low-loss SiN, with heterogeneous
integration of InP (SMART Photonics micro-transfer-printing), thin-film lithium niobate, and
germanium. Announced **June 2023**; opened for first prototyping **March 2024**. Consortium partners:
manufacturing/tech — X-FAB, LIGENTEC, SMART Photonics, PHIX, Luceda; research — **CEA-Leti, imec**;
application developers/end-users — **Nokia, NVIDIA, Thales, Aryballe, PhotonFirst, Brolis**. (Sivers
is *not* a member — the brief floated it; no evidence.) It is **prototyping/small-volume today**, with
"full-scale production in the future." Verified against photonixfab.eu, xfab.com (14-Jun-2023),
optics.org, imec (Mar-2024).

**The NVIDIA claim, decomposed:**

| Sub-claim | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| NVIDIA is a photonixFAB partner | **VERIFIED** | photonixfab.eu/project-partners; X-FAB PR 14-Jun-2023 |
| NVIDIA "in evaluation stage… for transceivers/optical switches" via photonixFAB | **VERIFIED (literal)** | Consortium text: NVIDIA "will use the photonixFAB platform to generate layouts for transceivers and optical switches and will evaluate the demonstrators on its testbed" |
| The relationship is "hidden" under the photonixFAB name | **REFUTED** | It has been openly published on X-FAB and consortium sites since 2023 |
| It implies NVIDIA **high-volume manufacturing** at X-FAB | **UNVERIFIED → effectively REFUTED** | NVIDIA's *production* CPO chain (Spectrum-X/Quantum-X, GTC Mar-2025) names **TSMC** (SiPh foundry + COUPE/SoIC packaging) + Browave, Coherent, Corning, Fabrinet, Foxconn, Lumentum, SENKO, SPIL, Sumitomo, TFC — **X-FAB/photonixFAB appear nowhere.** NVIDIA's MRM silicon-photonics engine is fabbed at TSMC. |

**The PATTERNS_AND_TRAPS direction test.** This is the *weak* direction. X-FAB/the consortium name
NVIDIA; **NVIDIA does not name X-FAB.** It is stronger than pure marketing adjacency — NVIDIA actually
signed onto an EU R&D project — but it is an **evaluation seat alongside Nokia/Thales/Aryballe**, not a
sourcing decision, and it is years and a full qualification cycle away from being a revenue event.
NVIDIA evaluating a *sovereign-European* photonics platform is a sensible optionality/resilience move
for NVIDIA; it is not evidence of a lead production path over TSMC.

**It's not even an exclusive signal.** **GlobalFoundries' Fotonix platform launched in March 2022
with NVIDIA, Broadcom, Cisco, and Marvell as named partners** — so NVIDIA is *publicly tied to a
competitor's SiPh platform too*. And the *better-documented* "NVIDIA names a merchant SiPh foundry"
trade is **Tower (TSEM)**, which on **5-Feb-2026** announced an NVIDIA collaboration for 1.6T SiPh
optical modules "designed for NVIDIA networking protocols" — and even *that* is Tower-side language,
not an NVIDIA-endorsed production award. The strongest available version of this trade lives at TSEM,
not X-FAB.

**Current SiPh customers / revenue.** Per X-FAB's **own FY2024 annual report**, silicon-photonics
"revenue contribution in 2024 has been small" and **is not broken out as a line item.** Named SiPh
engagements (LIGENTEC, SMART Photonics) are R&D collaborations, not disclosed volume production.
*(Caveat: X-FAB reports a "Microsystems & Photonics" technology line that hit a record $33.7M in
Q1-2026 (+42% YoY) — but that line is dominated by MEMS, §7, and "photonics" in X-FAB's taxonomy is
broader than datacom silicon-photonics. Datacom-SiPh-for-AI specifically is immaterial/pre-revenue
today. Do not read the $33.7M as a SiPh-for-NVIDIA number.)*

**Competitive position.** X-FAB is a **small, niche, 200mm, prototyping-stage** merchant SiPh player.
The volume SiPh-for-AI-datacom market is **TSMC, Tower (PH18, 300mm), and GlobalFoundries (Fotonix,
scaling aggressively — bought Advanced Micro Foundry Nov-2025, targeting $1B SiPh revenue)** plus AIM
Photonics — together >60% of specialized photonics wafer contracts. X-FAB differentiates on European
sovereignty + heterogeneous integration, not scale, and is clearly *behind* on volume SiPh.

**The TSEM precedent (the brief's analog).** Verified: **Intel agreed to buy Tower for $5.4B
(Feb-2022); the deal was TERMINATED 16-Aug-2023** (failed to obtain China SAMR approval; Intel paid a
$353M break fee). Tower subsequently re-rated as a recognized SiPh/CPO name (and now trades ~4.8x P/B
/ ~24–27x EV/EBITDA). **Is there an analogous catalyst path for X-FAB?** Possible but not visible — and
**family control (§12) makes a clean acquisition far harder than TSEM's setup**; no strategic stake or
M&A catalyst is announced.

**Verdict on the most-important leg:** the NVIDIA–X-FAB link is **real, small, public, and R&D-stage**
— optionality, not a thesis. The "hidden high-volume NVIDIA supplier" framing should be **dropped**.

## 5. The SiC / 800 VDC power-semiconductor thesis — one solid fact (POWI), one mis-stated fact (it's silicon, not SiC), one refuted claim (Navitas), one self-marketed claim ("only US SiC foundry").

X-FAB *is* a genuine SiC foundry — **150mm (6-inch) only, run out of Lubbock, Texas** (not Erfurt;
Erfurt = MEMS/SOI/HQ, Dresden = GaN). It offers epitaxy, high-temp implantation, ohmic contacts,
backside metal, thinning to ~110µm, and planar SBD/MPS/JBS/MOSFET/JFET device tech; a real, named
third-party SiC customer is **UnitedSiC (now Qorvo)**. So the "fabs SiC for others in the US" model is
real. **But the financial reality is tiny and was shrinking:** SiC revenue **FY2025 = $33.8M, ~4% of
total, DOWN 34% YoY** on the industry-wide SiC inventory correction (X-FAB *halted* SiC capacity
expansion in 2024). Q4-25 rebounded +77% YoY and Q1-26 wide-bandgap hit $15.1M (+152% YoY) — but off a
low base, with SiC utilization ~60% (≈6k of 10k wafers/month). X-FAB's *own* Q4-25 commentary cites
"data center, EV, renewable energy" as SiC drivers — that one line is the strongest *direct* link to
the AI/datacenter theme, and it is thin.

**The four sub-claims, verified:**

- **POWI "names X-FAB as its foundry in PCN-22181" → PARTIAL, and the brief mis-states the chemistry.**
  The **X-FAB↔Power Integrations foundry relationship is rock-solid**: every POWI 10-K from 2007 through
  the latest (filed **2026-02-06**) names "X-FAB Semiconductor Foundries AG" as one of three wafer
  foundries (with Lapis and Epson), and the X-FAB wafer-supply agreement now runs to **December 2028.**
  **CRITICAL CORRECTION:** POWI's X-FAB wafers are **high-voltage *SILICON*** (POWI's proprietary HV/BCD
  process for AC-DC ICs and gate drivers) — **NOT SiC.** POWI's GaN ("PowiGaN") foundry is *undisclosed*,
  and POWI runs no SiC of its own. So "POWI uses X-FAB for SiC chips for 800 VDC" is **not supported** —
  it uses X-FAB for legacy HV silicon. The specific document **"PCN-22181" could not be located** —
  treat that citation as **unconfirmed**; the underlying foundry fact is true, the SiC framing is wrong.

- **NVTS (Navitas) "relationship with X-FAB for GaN/SiC" → REFUTED / UNDOCUMENTED.** No documented
  X-FAB↔Navitas foundry deal exists. Navitas's *named* foundries are **TSMC, PSMC (200mm GaN), and
  GlobalFoundries (US GaN, announced 20-Nov-2025)**; its SiC (GeneSiC) is externally foundried with no
  X-FAB on record. The likely source of confusion: X-FAB launched a *generic* GaN-on-Si d-mode foundry
  offering (**XG035, 200mm, Dresden, Sept-2025**) open to any customer — it names no customer, certainly
  not Navitas. **This leg should be dropped unless a primary source surfaces.**

- **"Only high-volume SiC foundry in the US" → PARTIAL, and it's X-FAB's own marketing.** Lubbock genuinely
  runs 150mm SiC production. The phrase originates from **X-FAB's own careers page**, not an independent
  source. It is *defensible* if you narrowly define "pure-play SiC **foundry** (fabs for others)" —
  Wolfspeed, onsemi, and Coherent/II-VI have US SiC fabs but are **IDMs** (make their own chips);
  GlobalFoundries isn't positioned as a pure-play SiC foundry. So "only pure-play SiC foundry in the US"
  is plausibly true — **but it's self-asserted, and X-FAB's *headline* marketing claim ("first pure-play
  foundry for SiC and GaN") is global, with the "only in the US" being the careers-page narrowing.**

- **800 VDC / Vera Rubin chain → a three-link inference with a broken middle link.** NVIDIA's **13-Oct-2025
  800 VDC reference architecture** names *silicon providers* — ADI, **AOS (Alpha & Omega)**, EPC, Infineon,
  Innoscience, MPS, **Navitas**, onsemi, **Power Integrations**, Renesas, Richtek, ROHM, ST, TI — and
  Navitas separately announced direct 800V HVDC work with NVIDIA. **X-FAB appears nowhere** (it's a foundry,
  not a chip brand, so it never would). The inference "those NVIDIA-named designers fab their 800VDC chips
  at X-FAB" is **unproven and partly false**: POWI's X-FAB wafers are legacy HV silicon (not the GaN/SiC
  that goes into 800V conversion), Navitas↔X-FAB is undocumented, and AOSL has its own Oregon fabs. The
  picks-and-shovels conclusion **does not follow from verified facts.**

**Verdict on the SiC/800V leg:** real foundry, real POWI relationship, real US SiC line — but the *AI-800V
beneficiary* story is narrative built on inference, the chemistry is mis-stated (silicon, not SiC), one
named customer (Navitas) is undocumented, and SiC is ~4% of revenue and was *down 34%.* The upside is real
but tiny relative to the company today.

## 7. MEMS — the genuine, growing, under-appreciated leg (more real than the AI legs).

X-FAB is one of the few global merchant **MEMS** foundries, and this is the leg the brief under-weights
and the chokepoint framing over-weights elsewhere. **Microsystems (MEMS) revenue exceeded $100M for the
first time in FY2025 (+11% YoY)** — roughly **~12% of total revenue and growing**, in direct contrast to
the shrinking SiC line. Products span pressure sensors and strain gauges (the base), inertial sensors,
micro-mirrors, RF MEMS, and microfluidic MEMS with CMOS underneath, plus wafer-level packaging/TSV; a
notable FY2025/Q1-26 development is a new **piezo-MEMS line making scandium-doped aluminum-nitride (AlScN)
thin films for a (named-only-as "key") inkjet-printhead customer.** Two MEMS sites: **Erfurt** (X-FAB MEMS
Foundry GmbH) and **Itzehoe** (MFI, fully acquired 2015, 8-inch). Customer names are not disclosed.

**Read:** MEMS is a real, differentiated, double-digit-growing franchise and one of the strongest *actual*
parts of the X-FAB optionality story — quietly more bankable than the SiPh/SiC-AI legs that got the stock
moving. It is also the target of the €128M EU Chips Act grant (§8, Erfurt "Fab4Micro").

## The crux, made crisp

Everything the fraud gate cares about is fine: X-FAB is a real, ~34-year-old, profitable, six-fab merchant
foundry with ~400 customers, no promotion footprint, and genuine government validation (€128M EU + a soft
$50M US). And it screens *cheap on assets* — ~1.3–1.5x book and ~9–11x EV/EBITDA, the lowest of its peer set,
with a real **2026 FCF inflection** as a $510M→$100M capex cliff plays out, plus a quietly strong, growing
**MEMS** franchise (>$100M, +11%) and embedded optionality in SiC and silicon-photonics. The **Soitec
structural parallel is genuine** — legacy weakness (automotive) masking emerging high-growth segments.

**What separates it from an opportunity is honesty about four things.** *First,* the **three headline
chokepoint legs are mostly inference**: NVIDIA is one of several R&D *evaluators* on the photonixFAB
consortium (publicly documented, not hidden — and NVIDIA's actual silicon-photonics production runs through
TSMC, with GlobalFoundries' Fotonix *also* naming NVIDIA); POWI's verified X-FAB foundry relationship is
**high-voltage silicon, not SiC**; the **Navitas link is undocumented**; and the "only US SiC foundry" line is
X-FAB's own marketing. *Second,* the **growth engines are tiny today** — SiC is ~4% of revenue and was **down
34%**; datacom silicon-photonics is immaterial/pre-revenue. *Third,* the **core is in a cyclical/concentration
trough that halved earnings** — ~61% automotive, ~45% one family-affiliated customer (Melexis), utilization in
the low 60s, no full-year 2026 guidance, the $1.5B/35% target slid from 2026 to 2030. *Fourth,* the **stock has
already run ~4x (€4.09→€15.88) and sits mid-pullback at ~€10.71 / ~50x trailing earnings while the Street is
cutting estimates and every aggregated target sits *below* the price** — and **family control + Melexis
relatedness + a ~61%-locked register cap both the clean re-rate and any M&A path**, with the whole position
reachable only on Paris via IBKR (equity-only; the OTC line is dead).

**Net: a legitimate cyclical-recovery-plus-optionality name — real company, clean fraud gate, genuinely cheap
on assets/EBITDA, real MEMS growth, real government backing, a credible 2026 FCF turn, and a real Soitec-style
structural setup — but NOT the hidden-NVIDIA-supplier moonshot the brief frames.** The bull case is operating
leverage on an automotive recovery (~1.5–2.5x to and beyond the old high), the base case is a churn around the
current price as FCF turns, and the bear case is a round-trip to €5–7 if the auto/Melexis trough deepens and
the narrative premium deflates. The honest entry is **on a deeper pullback (toward where the asset value and
the sell-side actually sit), or on first *hard proof* the automotive cycle has turned and MEMS/SiC/photonics
are converting to a visible second revenue leg** — not chasing the post-run name at ~50x earnings on a
chokepoint story that the filings don't yet support.
