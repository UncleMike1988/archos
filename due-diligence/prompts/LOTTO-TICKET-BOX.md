## THE GOAL

Find real companies trading at sub-$5M market cap (as low as $50K)
that were once worth $50M+ and have a credible signal that someone
is trying to fix them. These are $1K lottery tickets — we expect
most to go to zero, but a single 100-500x rebound pays for all losses.

The edge: no human can efficiently sort through thousands of sub-$5M
names to distinguish real distressed companies from hollow shells
and promotions. You can.

## PHASE 1 — BUILD THE UNIVERSE

This is the hardest part. You need to find companies that meet ALL of:

a) Currently listed on a US exchange or OTC Markets (must be tradeable
   on a standard US brokerage)
b) Current market cap under $5M (the lower the better — $50K to $5M
   is the sweet spot)
c) Was worth $50M+ market cap at some point in the last 7 years
   (proves it was once a legitimate business)
d) Still files with the SEC (current on 10-K or 10-Q within the
   last 12 months — if filings stopped, it's a dead shell, SKIP)

HOW TO FIND THEM:
- Search SEC EDGAR for companies with recent filings (last 12 months)
  that have very small current market caps
- Search for recent reverse splits (8-K filings with "reverse stock
  split" in the last 6 months) — these are often sub-$5M companies
  trying to maintain listing compliance
- Search for companies that received Nasdaq/NYSE deficiency notices
  (8-K filings mentioning "listing standards" or "compliance" or
  "minimum bid price" in the last 6 months)
- Search for recent CEO/board appointment 8-Ks from very small filers
- Search OTC Markets for companies in the "Pink Current" tier (still
  filing, still reporting, but micro-cap)
- Cross-reference: find companies where the 52-week high is 10x+
  above the current price (collapse indicator)
- Look at companies emerging from Chapter 11 bankruptcy in the last
  12 months with new equity issued

Cast the widest possible net. You should find 50-200 names in the
raw universe. Quality filtering comes in Phase 2.

## PHASE 2 — THE "REAL COMPANY" FILTER

For every name from Phase 1, verify ALL of these:

1. WAS IT EVER A REAL BUSINESS?
   - Had annual revenue of $10M+ in at least one of the last 7 years
   - Had real employees (not just officers — check 10-K employee count)
   - Had real operations (products shipped, services delivered)
   - If it was always a development-stage company that never had
     revenue, SKIP — we want fallen angels, not failed startups

2. IS IT STILL A LEGAL ENTITY THAT FILES?
   - 10-K or 10-Q filed within the last 12 months
   - No SEC trading suspension (check SEC litigation releases)
   - No delinquent filer status
   - If the most recent filing is >12 months old, SKIP

3. DOES IT HAVE A SURVIVAL PATH?
   - Cash on hand vs quarterly burn rate = runway in months
   - If runway is <6 months AND no financing announced, flag as
     HIGH RISK (don't auto-kill — some of these raise last-minute)
   - Any debt that could trigger forced liquidation?
   - Going concern opinion from auditor? (flag, don't auto-kill)

4. KILL SIGNALS — HARD REJECT if ANY of these are true:
   - Death spiral / toxic convertible notes (variable-rate converts
     where the conversion price drops with the stock — the CYCU pattern)
   - CEO or board members with a history of involvement in 3+ other
     penny stock companies (serial shell promoters — check backgrounds)
   - SEC enforcement action (litigation release) against the company
     or current officers
   - Chinese reverse merger shell structure
   - No discernible business operations (pure holding company with
     no operating subsidiary generating revenue)
   - Active pump-and-dump indicators (paid stock promotion campaigns,
     OTC Markets "Caveat Emptor" flag)
   - Company has done 3+ reverse splits in the last 5 years (chronic
     diluter that keeps shrinking shareholders)

## PHASE 3 — THE "SOMEONE IS TRYING TO FIX IT" SIGNAL

For every survivor from Phase 2, look for at least ONE of these
recovery signals (ranked by strength):

STRONGEST SIGNALS:
A) New CEO appointed in the last 6 months who came from a credible
   company (verify their LinkedIn/background — did they run a real
   business before, or are they a penny stock career officer?)
B) New board member with genuine industry expertise or institutional
   investor background (activist investor, PE firm, industry veteran)
C) Open-market insider buying by officers/directors in the last 90
   days (Form 4 — must be actual purchases, NOT comp grants/RSUs)
D) Emerged from Chapter 11 bankruptcy with a clean balance sheet
   and new management team

MODERATE SIGNALS:
E) New strategic partnership or customer contract announced via 8-K
   (verify the partner is real and the contract has dollar amounts)
F) Reverse split completed + Nasdaq/NYSE compliance regained
   (signals intent to maintain legitimate listing status)
G) Asset sale or restructuring that simplified the business
   (divested the bad division, kept the good one)
H) New financing from a reputable source (not a toxic lender —
   verify the lender's track record)

WEAK SIGNALS (not sufficient alone):
I) Press release about a "strategic review" or "transformation plan"
   (words are cheap — need action behind them)
J) Hired a new IR firm or auditor upgrade (small positive, but
   cosmetic without substance)

Each survivor gets tagged with which signals it has (A-J) and the
strength tier.
