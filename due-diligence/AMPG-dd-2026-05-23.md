## Executive verdict

**Overall DD verdict: PROCEED WITH CAUTION — 0 RED / 4 FLAG / 2 CLEAR.**

AMPG is materially different from the SHAZ profile in every load-bearing
dimension: a real 14-year operating business with audited $25M FY25 revenue,
zero long-term debt, named investment-grade counterparties (Telus identified
as the end customer for the $78M LOI, plus a separate $40M LOI with a
Tier-1 North American MNO), credible institutional ownership (Vanguard 5.76%,
Bard Associates 6.87%), real Maxim Group sell-side coverage with a transparent
PT cut for dilution, and an identifiable supply chain with named real
customers (Lockheed Martin, L3Harris, Viasat, CPI). The hard `RED` triggers
that defined SHAZ (counterparty insolvency, fake DeFi lender, supervoting
entrenchment, published short report with verified claims, IPO underwriter
selling shares) are all ABSENT.

But four specific FLAGs require explicit risk acceptance and ongoing
monitoring before any position-sizing decision:

1. **Material weaknesses in internal controls disclosed in both the FY25 10-K
   and the Q1 26 10-Q** — CEO and CFO concluded that disclosure controls AND
   internal control over financial reporting are NOT EFFECTIVE due to lack of
   written policies, insufficient accounting personnel, and ineffective IT
   general controls. These weaknesses are the same root cause that enabled
   the $3.25M crypto-phishing scam loss in Q1 2024.

2. **Severe customer concentration on a transitional reseller arrangement.**
   One customer = 42.86% of FY25 revenue ($10.8M of $25.2M) and "was not a
   customer of the Company prior to the asset acquisition" — meaning the
   entire 5G ORAN business is acquired-IP-and-pre-existing-purchase-orders
   from Titan Crest LLC, fulfilled through a transitional supply-chain
   arrangement that compresses gross margin. Supplier A = 58.28% of FY25
   material purchases ($10.2M of $17.5M) — likely Titan or a Titan-related
   manufacturer. The 5G ORAN business is essentially AMPG-as-middleman
   between Titan (the manufacturer) and Telus (the end customer), with
   AMPG owning the IP and the customer relationship but not (yet) the
   production line.

3. **Aggressive equity issuance pattern with a placement-agent / sell-side
   analyst overlap.** AMPG has executed 8+ registered direct offerings,
   ATMs, and rights offerings since November 2024, including a $0.7302/share
   single-investor RDO in September 2024 when the stock was below $1, and a
   high-cost ($21.2% interest) Altbanq business loan in July 2024. Shares
   outstanding grew from 19.66M (12/31/2024) to 25.34M (5/11/2026), a
   28.9% YoY dilution. Maxim Group is simultaneously the (i) sell-side
   covering analyst (Buy / $7 PT, cut from $9 explicitly for dilution),
   (ii) ATM exclusive sales agent (3.0% fee on up to $25M), and (iii)
   placement agent on multiple historical RDOs (6.5–7.0% fees). This is a
   weaker SHAZ-Lucid pattern (Maxim is at least transparent and the PT was
   cut not raised), but the conflict exists.

4. **The $100M "previously announced" LOI in the Jan 5, 2026 press release
   reconciles to a $78M LOI in the FY25 10-K filed 2.5 months later.** The
   discrepancy is unexplained. The Nov 3, 2025 Free Writing Prospectus
   reconciles cleanly: $78M LOI ("major global 5G ORAN organization,"
   end customer Telus per the March 2025 8-K) + $40M LOI ("Tier-1 North
   American mobile network operator") = $118M total. The Jan 5, 2026
   "$100M" framing appears to be casual marketing rounding rather than a
   specific contractual document. Not a SHAZ-style retraction, but a
   precision-discipline gap.

The non-FLAG positives are real and substantial: confirmed Telus
counterparty via the March 31, 2025 Titan APA 8-K (Telus is named four times
explicitly before being anonymized to "Third Party Customer" in the FY25
10-K), zero published short reports, very low 3.94% short interest, no

filed 2026-05-22 by Daniel Mazziota for ~$49,500 (all from equity
compensation grants), no class actions, no related-party transactions
disclosed, no RedChip paid-promotion disclosure in any SEC filing (despite
