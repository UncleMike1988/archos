## 1. Is it real? — Clean pass on the fraud gate. But "real" ≠ "good," and the gap is the whole story.

**The one hard rule is an easy yes.** IQE is the opposite of a promotion: it **IPO'd on AIM in 2000**,
has a **~25-year public history**, runs **physical MOCVD/MBE epitaxy fabs** in Cardiff and Newport
(Wales), Greensboro (North Carolina), Taunton (Massachusetts) and Hsinchu (Taiwan), printed **£97.3M of
audited FY2025 revenue**, and carries an institutional register (Lombard Odier, T. Rowe Price, Artisan,
DFA/Avantis) plus a Nasdaq-listed strategic anchor (MACOM, ~$28–29B). There is **no paid-IR signature**
(no IBN/RedChip/MZ Group), no cashtag-bot spam, no death-spiral-converts-funding-a-deck. The bellwether
attribution runs the *right* direction — Coherent/Lumentum/Marvell naming the InP constraint, NVIDIA
writing the checks — and IQE sits genuinely under that layer. **Nothing here trips the promotion/fraud
test. Move on.**

**Where the honesty has to land is layer two: this is a real company that has been a chronically
*bad business*.** Don't let the clean fraud-gate pass smuggle in a quality halo it hasn't earned:

- **It loses money structurally.** FY2025 statutory operating loss **£(30.1)M** on £97.3M revenue;
  reported pre-tax loss **£(37.0)M**; reported diluted EPS **(3.77)p**; gross margin **~1.4%**. This is
  not a one-bad-year story — retained losses sit at **£(122.3)M**.
- **It just had to be rescued.** The £81M May-2026 recap (Section 6) repaid a $35M HSBC facility that was
  *classified current* (due within 12 months) and redeemed converts that were also current. Pre-raise
  cash was **£15.7M** against ~£47M of near-term bank+convert debt. The auditor's clean going-concern
  opinion is *predicated on the raise completing.* This was a lifeline, not a flex.
- **Its history is violently cyclical.** IQE's all-time high was ~**155–180p in November 2017** on
  Apple-Face-ID VCSEL euphoria (+314% that year); it then spent six years grinding to ~5p as that
  single-customer concentration whipsawed it. The Wireless segment alone went **+25% (FY24) → −40%
  (FY25).** "Real" has never been the question with IQE; "worth owning, when" is.

So the fraud gate is the wrong frame to dwell on. The real DD is whether a serially-loss-making,
just-recapitalized merchant epi house is *finally* at the inflection where the InP/AI-datacom mix
re-rates the whole company — or whether this is the 2017 VCSEL movie running again with a new
chemistry. Everything below is in service of that question.

## 3. The constraint — the most-corroborated fire of the cycle, and it is genuinely IQE's layer. (Verified, with the nuances.)

The InP constraint is the single strongest part of this thesis and it survived verbatim verification.
The exact, sourced bellwether record:

- **Coherent (COHR) — FQ3-26 call, 6 May 2026, CEO Jim Anderson:** *"Indium phosphide has been the key
  constraint for us for a number of quarters — it is a constraint for the industry."* On track to
  **double internal InP capacity by end-CY2026** (pulled forward a quarter), and to **"more than double
  again by end-CY2027"**; its 6-inch InP platform is producing **EMLs, CW lasers and photodiodes** at
  yields above its 3-inch lines. *(Nuance vs the hunt framing: it's total internal InP capacity by
  end-2026, not "6-inch by next quarter.")*
- **Lumentum (LITE) — FQ3-26 call, ~5 May 2026, CEO Michael Hurlston:** components *"effectively sold
  out for the foreseeable future"*; the **supply-demand imbalance is ">30%"**; *"we are significantly
  undershipping demand… having to make choices as to who we support."* **200G EML *revenue* more than
  doubled sequentially**, with >50% EML *unit* growth guided into Dec-2026. Record quarter, ~$808M
  revenue, +90% YoY. (Note: Lumentum is **converting its newly-acquired Greensboro, NC fab from GaAs to
  InP** — relevant to the captive-epi risk in Section 5.)
- **Marvell (MRVL) — Q1-FY27 call, 27 May 2026, COO Chris Koopmans:** *"I don't think we've been in an
  unconstrained environment since [2020/2021]… Everything that touches AI has been constrained."* CFO:
  **~$1B of supplier prepayments** this fiscal year; CEO: five-year forecasts to "a small number of key
  suppliers." **Caveat (important, do not overstate):** this is a *general* AI-supply comment — the
  Marvell transcript **does not mention indium phosphide at all.** It corroborates "all AI optical supply
  is tight + primes are prepaying to lock it," *not* InP specifically.
- **NVIDIA optical lock-up — $4B equity hard-confirmed (not ~$6B):** NVIDIA bought **$2.0B of Coherent**
  (7,788,161 shares @ $256.80) and **$2.0B of Lumentum**, both announced **2 Mar 2026**, each bundled
  with multi-year supply agreements and *multi-billion (unquantified) purchase commitments.* The "~$6B"
  in the hunt note only holds if you add those undisclosed purchase commitments to the $4B equity — so
  **$4B equity confirmed, ~$6B is plausible-but-unquantified.**

**The demand numbers (LightCounting, the primary forecaster):** AI Ethernet optics + CPO **~$16.5B
(2025) → ~$26B (2026)**; Ethernet-transceiver growth +93% (2024) → ~+82% (2025e) → ~+65% (2026e), with
**2026 growth explicitly "limited by the production capacity of InP EML and laser chips."** The physics
driving it: AI scale-out moved the optics mix from short-reach 850nm GaAs VCSELs to **single-mode
1310/1550nm DR/FR/LR links and silicon-photonics/CPO light sources — all InP** (EMLs, CW DFB lasers,
InGaAs photodiodes). The **800G→1.6T transition roughly doubles InP content per module** (4 lanes → 8).

**How long does it last?** This is the honest governor on the thesis: LightCounting's base case is the
*merchant-transceiver* shortage **easing by end-2026, possibly stretching into 2027** if GPU supply
extends it — i.e. a **cyclical-to-structural ~18-month window, not a permanent moat.** Coherent's
"double again by 2027" capacity language says the *primes themselves* expect tightness into 2027 and are
**building to kill it.** That cuts both ways: the fire is real and dated, but the people naming it are
also racing to extinguish it (in-house — see Section 5).

## 4. IQE's place in the chain + named customers — the strong part of the thesis and the weakest part, in the same section.

**What IQE actually is (confirmed):** a **merchant epitaxial-wafer foundry.** It buys *bare substrates*
(InP/GaAs/GaN/GaSb boules-and-wafers from Sumitomo / AXT-Tongmei / JX) and **grows the epitaxial device
layers** on them via MOCVD and MBE, then sells the finished *epi-wafer* to laser/PIC/chip makers who
fabricate the device. It is **not** a substrate maker (that's the layer AXT plays in) and **not** a
finished-laser maker (that's the customer). IQE's genuine differentiation is **breadth** — it claims to
be the only supplier across *both* major epitaxy platforms (MOCVD + MBE) with the broadest III-V
materials portfolio (GaAs/InP/GaN/GaSb) — and it has grown InP epi since inception.

**The critical weakness — IQE does NOT separately disclose InP-datacom revenue.** The Photonics segment
(**£57.1M, +15%, now the larger segment**) is a *bundle* of (a) GaAs **VCSEL 3D-sensing** (the legacy
Apple/consumer business), (b) **InP datacom** (the AI story), and (c) **US defence/sensing** programs
(which drove much of the H2-2025 growth via funding releases). **So the actual InP-AI-datacenter revenue
today is undisclosed and almost certainly still small** — the thesis rests on a *forward* mix shift, not
a demonstrated InP-datacom revenue base. This is the most important single fact in the file and it is
easy to miss inside the clean "Photonics +15%" headline.

**Named customers — confirmed vs inferred, brutally honest (this is where the thesis is softest):**

- **Lumentum — CONFIRMED customer, but for the *wrong product*.** IQE's own press release (8 Dec 2025)
  *"extends multi-year strategic supply agreement with Lumentum"* — but the disclosed scope is **VCSEL
  epi for 3D-sensing** (consumer, automotive LiDAR, in-cabin), i.e. the *legacy GaAs Face-ID-adjacent*
  business, **NOT** InP datacom. Any claim that "IQE supplies Lumentum's AI-datacenter InP lasers" is
  **inferred and unsupported by the public record.** The one marquee buyer-named relationship is on the
  declining-legacy side of the house, not the AI side.
- **MACOM — CONFIRMED customer + ~11.5% anchor shareholder + supply partner; product scope undisclosed.**
  The May-2026 recap bundled **Long-Term Strategic Supply Agreements (LTSAs)** for "epitaxial services
  across multiple technologies," and MACOM CEO Stephen Daly called IQE *"a longstanding customer."*
  MACOM is a genuine InP/photonics player (it bought BinOptics, an InP-laser house, for $230M in 2014),
  so MACOM buying InP epi from IQE is commercially logical **and now contractually committed** — but the
  **LTSA product scope is not disclosed** (could be InP, GaAs and/or GaN). So "MACOM buys IQE *InP
  datacom* epi" is **probable-but-unconfirmed.** This is nonetheless the strongest validation on the
  file: a real strategic buyer put in capital, took board seats, and locked volume (Section 6).
- **Coherent — INFERRED, and most likely a *competitor*, not a customer.** No source names IQE as a
  Coherent supplier, and Coherent runs **captive InP epitaxy** (Section 5) — it grows its own. Treat
  "IQE supplies Coherent" as **unsupported and contra-indicated.**
- **Broadcom/Avago — INFERRED legacy concentration, never IQE-named.** Widely reported as IQE's largest
  historical customer (GaAs RF + VCSELs into Apple Face ID); the source of the notorious
  customer-concentration whiplash (Wireless −40% in FY25). Exact % undisclosed but real.
- **The InP-datacom roster itself — UNNAMED "Tier-1 design wins," pre-volume.** IQE's FY25 RNS cites
  *"multiple Tier-1 InP photonics design wins"* and *"Tier-1 customer design wins for laser and detector
  products enabling next-generation AI and hyperscale data-centre infrastructure."* This is the *weakest*
  form of attribution: company-side, unnamed, design-win (pre-revenue) stage, no buyer confirming, no
  revenue disclosed.

**Net:** the supply-chain *position* is real and correctly placed under the fire; the *customer proof*
for the specific InP-AI-datacom thesis is, today, a confirmed-customer-for-the-wrong-product (Lumentum),
a confirmed-anchor-with-undisclosed-scope (MACOM), and a basket of unnamed design wins. Buyer-side
attribution for the actual thesis is thin.

## 5. Competition + the captive-epi moat question — the central competitive risk, stated plainly.

**The single most important competitive fact: the two biggest InP laser makers grow their own InP epi
in-house.** Coherent performs **epitaxy and device fab in-house**, buying only the bare *substrate*
externally, and is building the world's first 6-inch InP epi lines (Sherman TX + Järfälla, Sweden +
Zurich by early 2027). Lumentum is **largely vertically integrated and is converting its Greensboro fab
to grow its own InP.** **Implication:** the richest slice of the InP-datacom cycle (the epitaxy itself)
is being **captured in-house by the very primes naming the shortage** — which structurally *shrinks the
merchant epi TAM* IQE is counting on. IQE is a **merchant supplier to customers who increasingly
self-supply.** Its realistic merchant lane is the players *without* captive epi — **MACOM** (now an
anchor/partner), smaller PIC/laser houses, and defence — not Coherent and not, over time, Lumentum's
InP. This is the deepest risk in the thesis and it is not priced as a risk in the bull narrative.

**Merchant InP-epi peers (the competitive set IQE actually fights):**
- **VPEC / Visual Photonics Epitaxy** (Taiwan, TWSE 2455) — the closest direct peer; makes InP/InGaAs
  photodiode and laser epi for 800G, guiding double-digit optoelectronic growth on 800G InP PD orders.
- **IntelliEPI** (Texas + Taiwan) — merchant GaAs/InP epi for telecom/photonics/RF.
- **LandMark Optoelectronics** (Taiwan) — epi + InP optical devices for datacom.
- **WIN Semiconductors** (Taiwan; world's largest GaAs foundry) — primarily GaAs; its role as a *volume
  merchant InP-epi* supplier is **unconfirmed** — likely peripheral to InP datacom.
- **Soitec** (Euronext) — makes engineered InP **substrates** via Smart-Cut layer transfer; this is the
  *substrate* layer (upstream of IQE), **not epi** — a potential input/competitor-by-adjacency, not a
  head-to-head epi rival. (The hunt list's instinct that Soitec is "the cleaner InP pure-play" is right
  on the *substrate* axis, wrong to equate with IQE's epi layer.)

**Is the merchant position defensible?** Partially, and honestly. *For it:* genuine MOCVD+MBE breadth,
decades of InP epi, a freshly-recapitalized balance sheet, and a strategic InP-buyer anchor (MACOM) with
board seats and locked volume. *Against it:* the highest-value InP-datacom customers self-supply;
multiple lower-cost Taiwan merchant peers; the marquee *named* relationship (Lumentum) is consumer VCSEL
not InP; and there is **no named InP-datacom *volume* customer yet** — only design wins. On today's
public record, the InP-datacom upside is a real *forward option*, not a confirmed, defended revenue line.

## 6. The MACOM recap, acquisition risk, and the uplisting question — terms, structure, and what it means.

**The £81M recapitalization (announced 27 Apr 2026, completed 28 May 2026) — exact structure, all at
19.8p:**
- **MACOM: £45M total** = **£30M equity (151,515,151 shares @ 19.8p, ≈11.5% of enlarged votes)** + **£15M
  zero-coupon secured convertible loan notes** (60-month term, conversion at 19.8p) + **warrants over
  75,757,575 shares** (exercisable only if the CLNs are redeemed early without conversion). In exchange,
  MACOM entered **Long-Term Strategic Supply Agreements** with IQE and gained the right to **two
  non-executive board seats for 30 months** (named: Robert Dennehy, MACOM COO; David O'Carroll, MACOM VP
  — pending Nomad DD at run time).
- **Existing-noteholder reinvestment: £22.8M** (115,011,962 shares) — redeeming the March-2025 converts
  at a 9% premium and rolling into equity.
- **Placing £11M** (55,555,555 shares) + **Retail offer up to £2M** (10,101,010 shares); **Peel Hunt**
  sole bookrunner.
- **Pricing: 19.8p = a 58.4% discount to the 47.6p close (24 Apr 2026)** and ~10.1% to the 12-month VWAP.
  The **332,183,678 new shares ≈ 25.3% of the enlarged base / ~+33% dilution** of the prior count.
- **Use of proceeds:** repays the **$35M (£27M) HSBC RCF in full** and redeems the prior converts,
  leaving a **~£27.9M net cash inflow.** Pro-forma the balance sheet swings from ~£31.5M adjusted net
  debt to roughly **~£22M net cash** (Peel Hunt est., ex-leases). *Watch the embedded ratchet:* the
  existing-noteholder warrant instrument carries **anti-dilution adjustment** that re-prices downward on
  discounted issuance like this — a latent further-dilution overhang worth quantifying from the Annual
  Report.

**What it means:** This is a **deeply-discounted solvency rescue executed by the company's own
longstanding customer** — simultaneously the most reassuring and least flattering thing on the file.
Reassuring: a sophisticated strategic (MACOM, ~40x IQE's size) underwrote survival, locked supply, took
board seats and put real capital in at the bottom. Unflattering: it took a **58% discount** and **~33%
dilution** to get done, and it followed a *failed* sale process.

**Acquisition risk — IQE was literally for sale and no acceptable bid emerged.** The strategic review
(announced 18 Nov 2024) **expanded on 8 Sep 2025 to "all options including a full sale of the Group,"**
putting IQE into a **City Code offer period** with a disclosed "approach from a potential offeror" and
"discussions with a number of potential bidders." On 27 Apr 2026 **"all discussions with third parties
for the whole of the Group" were terminated** and the MACOM recap was chosen instead. **Read-through:**
the company was shopped, found no full-takeover bid it would accept, and recapitalized to stay
independent. Going forward, **MACOM is the most logical eventual acquirer** (longstanding customer,
~11.5% toehold, two board seats, locked LTSAs, ~40x the size) — a classic toehold-plus-supply-lock
structure that *often* precedes a later bid — **but** MACOM holds **no control or blocking position**
(even on full CLN+warrant conversion it stays well under the **30% Rule 9 mandatory-bid threshold**),
there is **no stated intent to bid,** and Takeover-Code restrictions following the just-closed offer
period constrain any near-term move. *"MACOM as eventual acquirer" is an analytical inference, not a
sourced intention.* No specific Coherent/other named-bidder rumor surfaced.

**The uplisting question — answered honestly: NO signal.** There is **no evidence** IQE is considering a
US (Nasdaq/NYSE) listing, a dual listing, or even a move from AIM to the LSE Main Market. Every recent
action reaffirms AIM (the recap shares were admitted *to AIM*; IQE publishes under AIM Rule 26). The US
tickers **IQEPY (unsponsored ADR) and IQEPF (ordinary F-share) are OTC lines only, not an exchange
uplisting.** A US re-listing would be a powerful *future* re-rating catalyst (AIM small-caps chronically
trade at a discount to US comps — see AXT's $6.7B US cap on a smaller InP footprint), but **none is
signalled today.** File it as a hypothetical catalyst to watch, not a live one.

## 7. Balance sheet deep dive — the genuine hazard, pre- and post-rescue.

**Pre-raise (31 Dec 2025, as reported):** cash **£15.7M** (up from £4.7M); **HSBC bank borrowings £26.8M
(current)**; **convertible loan notes £20.3M (current)**; **lease liabilities £44.6M**. Headline
**"adjusted net debt £31.5M"** *excludes the leases* — on an IFRS-16-inclusive basis gross debt was
~£91.7M against £15.7M cash, i.e. **~£76M total net debt.** Total equity £89.7M; retained losses
**£(122.3)M.** The killer detail: **both the bank debt and the converts were *current*** (due within 12
months) — which is precisely what forced the recap. This was a company that *had* to raise.

**Cash burn / FCF (FY25):** reported operating cash flow **£8.1M**, capex **£(5.1)M** → simple FCF
**~£3.0M** *positive* — but that is **not durable quality**: it was driven by a working-capital release
(inventory drawn down, customers met from stock) and **capex halved YoY** (£11.4M → £5.1M). With FY26
guided to >20% revenue growth, **working capital reverses and capex likely rises** — the positive FCF is
a trough artifact, not a margin story. The *structural* problem was never opex burn; it was the **debt
maturity wall**, which the recap addressed.

**Post-raise pro-forma:** ~£44M cash (£15.7M + ~£27.9M net inflow), HSBC RCF gone, old converts redeemed,
remaining debt the **£15M MACOM CLN** (+ £44.6M leases) → **roughly ~£22M net cash ex-leases** (Peel Hunt).
**Survival is no longer the question** for the next couple of years *barring a capex ramp* — but note
the business is only ~cash-flow-neutral at the operating line, so the runway is "adequate," not "ample."

**Dilution arc:** ~801M shares (early 2021) → ~980M (2025) → **1,312,590,972 (Jun 2026)** — ~+64% over
five years, with the bulk in the deeply-discounted 2026 leg. A 25-year history of episodic dilution to
survive cyclical troughs is the pattern; the bull case requires this to be the *last* such raise.

**Governance flags:** **no permanent CFO in seat** (CEO Jutta Meier — ex-Intel, joined Jan 2024 as CFO,
took CEO in Oct 2024 after Americo Lemos departed immediately, confirmed in the role through FY25 — held
both hats; IQE says it intends to appoint a permanent CFO "in due course"). For a company this leveraged
and this dependent on a clean execution year, an unfilled CFO seat is a real, if fixable, flag.

## 8. Legacy RF/handset decline vs the InP crossover — the mix shift is real but partly mechanical.

| Segment | FY2024 | FY2025 | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wireless** (RF/handset; GaAs/GaN) | £67.3M (+25%) | **£40.1M (−40%)** | Legacy, lumpy, structurally declining; single-customer/handset inventory whiplash |
| **Photonics** (VCSEL 3D-sensing + InP datacom + defence) | £49.9M (−16%) | **£57.1M (+15%)** | Now the larger segment; growth from H2 US-defence funding + AI/datacom InP |
| **CMOS++** | £0.86M | £0.065M | Effectively wound down |
| **Total** | £118.0M | **£97.3M (−17.6%)** | Total revenue still *fell* — the inflection is FY26 *guidance*, not yet printed |

**The crossover (Photonics > Wireless) happened in FY2025 — but as much because Wireless *collapsed*
(−40%) as because Photonics *grew* (+15%).** Half the mix shift is the right reason (InP/defence growth),
half is the wrong reason (legacy decline). The bull thesis — Photonics/InP growing fast enough that
*total* company revenue inflects *up* — is **only guided to happen in FY2026** (the ">20%" total-company
guide back toward ~£117M, plus high-single/low-double-digit adjusted EBITDA). **FY25 total revenue fell;
the inflection is a forward promise.** When does InP growth durably overtake the RF drag at the company
level? On management's own numbers, **FY2026 is the test year.** That makes the FY26 prints (and any
H2-2026 InP-datacom revenue disclosure) the single most important things to watch.

## 12. Revenue model & return shapes — what InP photonics has to do to justify $1B / $2B / $5B.

## 13. What kills it — the one or two things that break the thesis.

**1 — The InP-datacom revenue never shows up at volume, because the primes self-supply the epi.** This is
*the* thesis-breaker: if the highest-value InP-datacom epitaxy is captured in-house by Coherent and
(increasingly) Lumentum, IQE's "design wins" stall at the design-win stage and the merchant lane proves
too small/too contested (vs VPEC/IntelliEPI/Landmark) to move a £100M+ company. The named-customer proof

explicitly dated to ease into 2027, and the primes are racing to extinguish it themselves. **If InP epi
stays a design-win story rather than a disclosed volume line, the entire re-rate was narrative.**

**2 — The balance sheet forces another dilutive raise.** The company is ~cash-flow-neutral at the
operating line, has just diluted ~33% at a 58% discount to survive, has a 25-year habit of trough
raises, faces a working-capital reversal and capex ramp if FY26 growth is real, and still carries a
warrant anti-dilution ratchet. If FY26 disappoints even modestly, a fresh raise off a weak price is the
base-rate outcome — and this is an equity-only name, so there is no LEAPS to cap the downside.
