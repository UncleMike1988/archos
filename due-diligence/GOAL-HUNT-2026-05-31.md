## Phase 3 — The Final Three

### #1: SGHT — Sight Sciences, Inc. (Nasdaq)
**The "how did I not know about this?" name. Conviction: HIGHEST — but as a
real, ignored, unpriced *special situation* (realistic 2–4x), not a verified 10x.**

**What it is.** A commercial eye-surgery company: the OMNI minimally-invasive
glaucoma surgery (MIGS) system and the TearCare dry-eye platform. ~$77M FY25
revenue (Q1-26 +13% YoY, 86% gross margin), still pre-profit (FY25 net
−$38.4M; op burn ~$11–12M/qtr). Price $4.86, ~52.5M shares, **~$255M cap.**

**The hidden asset (verified to the dollar).** On Apr 26, 2024 a Delaware
federal jury found Alcon (a $30B+ competitor) **willfully infringed all three**
of Sight's glaucoma patents (US 8,287,482 / 9,370,443 / 11,389,328) with its
**Hydrus Microstent.** Final Judgment entered ~Apr 22, 2026: **$55.4M total**
($34.0M past damages + $14.7M supplemental + $6.8M interest) **plus a 10%
running royalty on Hydrus revenue through Nov 10, 2028.** Triangulating from the
court-disclosed damages (Alcon doesn't break Hydrus out), Hydrus runs
~$70–90M/yr → **the forward royalty ≈ $7–9M/yr, ~$18–24M undiscounted to 2028.**
Combined back-award + annuity ≈ **$75–80M pre-tax cash, ~30% of the entire
market cap** — and the Street credits it ~$0 because it's pending appeal.

**Bull / base / bear (sum-of-parts).**
- *Operating business:* ~$80M rev growing low-teens at 86% GM ≈ $240M at 3x
  sales today; a turnaround re-rate to 4–5x ≈ $320–400M.
- *Litigation asset:* ~$75–80M gross, risk/time-adjusted ≈ $30–50M PV — free
  today.
- **Bull (~$14–18, 3–4x):** revenue compounds past $100M *and* the appeal
  affirms / Alcon settles → ~$55M cash + a $7–9M/yr annuity lands, kills the
  dilution risk, funds the path to breakeven; market pays 5x sales.
- **Base (~$6.50–8.50, ~consensus $9 avg PT):** revenue hits the $83–89M guide,
  litigation stays "pending" and is only partly credited.
- **Bear (~$2.50–3, −40%+):** Federal Circuit reduces/vacates *or* Alcon's
  USPTO ex-parte reexams knock out claims → litigation → $0, and the operating
  business hits its ~2H27 cash wall and dilutes.

**What kills it.** Two independent invalidation paths on a clock: the Federal
Circuit appeal (12–24 mo, no cash until exhausted) AND Alcon's ex-parte reexams.
If the ~$85M cash (≈7 quarters) empties before the royalty arrives, SGHT dilutes
into the very window the thesis needs. Mitigants are real: willful finding on
*all three* patents, PTAB already denied all four IPRs (2023), verdict survived
JMOL. Affirmance of *liability* is more-likely-than-not; *damages* reduction on
appeal is common.

**How ignored.** Partly, not totally — 6 analysts, ~50% institutional, 2.3%
short. The inefficiency is specific: the litigation cash is *acknowledged but
discounted to ~zero pending appeal.* That's the edge.

**WINNER_UNIVERSE analog.** Closest to the **RLMD / beaten-down-survivor-with-a-
hidden-second-catalyst** shape and the **MP "authority-as-validator"** pattern —
here the validator is a federal court, and the "second catalyst" is a royalty on
a giant competitor's flagship. A special-situation, not a momentum moonshot.

### #2: MYO — Myomo, Inc. (NYSE American)
**Highest literal-10x *torque* of the hunt — but it's a re-acceleration bet, not
a fresh catalyst. Conviction: SPECULATIVE.**

**What it is.** Makes the **MyoPro** — an EMG-controlled powered robotic arm
brace that restores movement to a paralyzed/weakened arm (stroke, brachial-
plexus injury), ~$59K ASP, billed to insurers, ~68% gross margin. **~$40M cap**
(price ~$1.00, ~38.6M shares), **$41M FY25 revenue (+26%)** — so it trades
*below 1x sales.*

**The catalyst (real and structural — but already fired).** Effective Jan 1,
2024, CMS classified the MyoPro in the **Medicare Part B brace benefit
category** with two permanent HCPCS codes (**L8701 ~$33.5K, L8702 ~$65.9K**,
fees live Apr 1, 2024). This took the device from *no* Medicare path to a
permanent lump-sum reimbursement code — a durable, hard-to-reverse moat. Medicare
Part B is now 51% of revenue, MA another 19%.

**The honest problem.** That catalyst is ~2 years old and is what drove the +26%
in 2025. **Q1-26 revenue grew only +2.9% YoY**; management calls 2026 a
"transition year" (guide $43–46M, +5–12%) as they deliberately shift from
ad-driven to recurring O&P-channel + MyoConnect referral demand. So the thesis
is *not* "new catalyst" — it's "does the recurring-revenue pivot re-accelerate
the top line off a structural reimbursement base."

**Balance sheet — passes (the make-or-break gate).** $15.7M cash/ST investments
(3/31/26), op burn only **$2.2M/qtr** (~7 quarters runway), ~$13.7M Avenue term
loan (not toxic converts), **no going-concern.** Share count roughly *flat* at
~38M through 2025–26 — they raised at higher prices and are not grinding an ATM.
This is the cleanest finding: it survives 18 months without a forced raise.

**Insiders.** Genuine director cash-buying cluster: 7 open-market (code P) buys
last 90 days (Kirk, Manko ~201K sh, Getz) at $0.70–0.91, net +$323K. One CMO
sell into the bounce dents it slightly.

**Bull / base / bear (price ~$1.00).**
- **Bull (~$3–4, 3–4x; analyst high $10 = the 10x outlier):** revenue surprises
  to ~$60M+ as the recurring pivot works, burn → breakeven, re-rate to ~3x sales.
- **Base (~$1–1.50):** guide lands (~+8%), drifts with execution.
- **Bear (sub-$0.50):** transition year flops, revenue flattens, forced raise at
  a depressed price + NYSE-American $1 minimum-bid / reverse-split risk.

**What kills it.** The catalyst is in the rear-view mirror and growth already
decelerated to ~3%. If the pivot doesn't re-accelerate, you own a flat-growth,
cash-burning $40M medtech at ~1x sales with listing risk — a value trap. The
*torque* is why it's here (a $40M cap with $41M real revenue and a structural
Medicare lane is genuine asymmetry); the *timing of the re-acceleration* is the
bet.

**WINNER_UNIVERSE analog.** The **TOI / WW HC-reimbursement-pivot** shape — a
tiny, ignored, real medical name where a structural payer change (not a product
launch) resets the addressable market. TOI ran ~10–14x off ~$1 on exactly this
pattern *once the operating turn showed up in the prints.* MYO needs that turn.

### #3: SND — Smart Sand, Inc. (Nasdaq)
**The freshest non-consensus angle: a profitable sand miner that is secretly an
AI-data-center *power* play. Conviction: MODERATE (real, clean, but a
commodity-cyclical ceiling caps it at ~2–3x).**

**What it is.** Mines and ships **Northern White frac sand** (premium proppant)
plus mine-to-wellsite logistics (silos, conveyors). **~$208M cap, $330M TTM
revenue, GAAP-profitable, pays a dividend + new $20M buyback.** Trades **<1x
sales.**

**The catalyst the market is mispricing.** Q1-26 revenue +42% YoY ($93M). The
new demand driver isn't oil drilling — it's **natural gas**: LNG export buildout
and gas-fired power for AI data centers pull on the *same* premium sand that
feeds gas wells. The Street files SND under "dying oil cyclical" and won't
connect a sand miner to the AI-power backbone. Management guides 5–10% volume
growth with positive FCF and is returning cash — a profitable company acting
like one.

**Bull / base / bear.**
- **Bull (~2–3x):** gas-demand narrative gets recognized, the multiple re-rates
  from <1x toward sector peers, volumes compound on LNG + DC-power gas.
- **Base (~flat to +50%):** profitable, pays you to wait, drifts with the gas rig
  count and proppant pricing.
- **Bear (−30%+):** proppant is commodity-cyclical; a gas-price or completion-
  activity air-pocket compresses both volume and the multiple. Insiders are net
  sellers — a real flaw to weigh.

**What kills it / the ceiling.** It's still a commodity producer — the upside is
volume- and price-paced, not a step-function re-rate, so this is the *least*
10x-shaped of the three. It earns the slot because it's the cleanest combination
of *profitable + genuinely cheap + a real, current demand inflection + a
non-consensus "how did I not know" angle.* Bucket-2 grower with optionality, not
a Bucket-1 lottery ticket.

**WINNER_UNIVERSE analog.** The **POWL / BW "boring-industrial-suddenly-levered-
to-the-AI-power-constraint"** shape — except SND is *already profitable and
already cheap*, which lowers the torque but also the risk.

## What the hunt revealed about the current landscape

1. **The $1B ceiling is where this cycle's winners already are.** Repeatedly,
   the best "bear-thesis-breaking, revenue-inflecting" small-caps (AMBQ, INOD,
   OUST, USAS, ARDX, T1 Energy, KRMN) had *already crossed $1B on the move that
   proved them.* Under a strict sub-$1B rule, you are necessarily fishing either
   *earlier* (pre-inflection, "jam tomorrow" like GSIT) or in *special
   situations the screens can't see* (SGHT's off-balance-sheet royalty).

2. **The AI-semicap lane is picked over** (prior hunts: ASYS, AEHR, AXTI, ONTO,
   TLS, OCC, AOSL). Deliberately hunting *outside* it is where the fresh,
   surprising names came from — litigation assets, reimbursement moats, and
   second-order AI-power derivatives.

3. **Promotion density is high in this tape.** Multiple "10x" pitches were
   serial-rebrand shells or paid-IR jobs (NUAI/Alpha Compute down 99.94% since
   IPO; NXXT with a 173% APR loan; VWAV wire-syndicated; CAPS acquisition-
   manufactured growth). The fraud gate did real work — most apparent
   moonshots failed it.

4. **"Insider cluster" requires reading the transaction codes.** Two pitched
   "insider buying" signals (WYY, partly Myomo's officer side) dissolved on
   inspection — WYY's were code-A *grants*, not code-P *buys*. The real clusters
   (MYO directors, KRMD's full C-suite) are the ones that survived.

5. **Honest answer to the prompt:** there is no name in this verified set where a
   12-month *10x* is supported by the fundamentals. **SGHT is the name that earns
   the "how did I not know about this?"** reaction — a money-losing small medtech
   quietly holding a court-ordered 10% royalty on a $30B rival's flagship product,
   credited at zero. It is a real, cheap, ignored, unpriced-catalyst setup. It is
   most honestly a 2–4x with a free option — and that is what I'd put in front of
   you rather than overstate a 10x the data won't carry.
