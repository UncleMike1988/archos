# VPG — Vishay Precision Group, Inc. (NYSE)

Caps/prices web-triangulated and good to ~2 significant figures — re-check intraday before acting.*

**Snapshot.** ~$125/share (Fri 5/29 close $125.31), **~$1.67B market cap** (~13.3M total shares —
12.27M common @ 1 vote + 1.02M family-held Class B @ 10 votes). Free float ~11.8M shares — *genuinely
thin*. Cash ~$87M against ~$21M of financial debt → **~$65M net cash** (the 10-K's own table shows
$66.8M; counting operating leases pulls it toward ~$38M) → **EV ~$1.6B**. 52-week range **$24.89 –
$131.68**; trades ~5% below the high. The number the brief under-weighted: **VPG has roughly tripled
in three months** (~$600M cap in late February → ~$1.67B now) and **doubled in the last month alone**.
The setup is real — a humanoid-robotics design-in *plus* a base-business order inflection — but the
move has already happened, and the price now reflects it. This is the rare Archos Equities case where the hard
DD lands on *valuation*, not on whether the company is real.

---

## 1. Is it real? — Clean pass. Exactly as expected.

Never in doubt, and the fraud gate is over in two sentences: VPG is a 15-year-public NYSE manufacturer,
$307M in real revenue, audited (Deloitte-network firm), with physical factories in Israel, India,
Japan and the US and decades-old brands — **Micro-Measurements** foil strain gauges, **Bulk Metal Foil**
precision resistors, **DTS** ruggedized data-acquisition, KELK/Stress-Tek weighing. End markets are
diversified and credible: test & measurement, semiconductor equipment, avionics/military/space,
transportation, steel, medical, industrial. No single customer dominates.

- **Balance sheet — clean, full stop.** ~$87M cash, ~$21M drawn on a $75M revolver (matures 2029),
  **~$65M net cash**, current ratio 4.5x, share count flat-to-shrinking (13.65M → 13.30M weighted over
  four years). No convertibles, no death-spiral notes, no ATM, stock comp a trivial 0.6% of revenue.
- **Operators — builders, not promoters.** CEO **Ziv Shoshani** is ex-Vishay, nephew of founder Felix
  Zandman; the bench is a deep ex-Vishay-Intertechnology engineering and finance core. Continuity, not
  narrative-hopping.
- **Promotion check — entirely organic.** No IBN/RedChip/MZ Group footprint, no paid-placement
  disclosures, no cashtag-bot spam. Coverage and capital are real: Dimensional, BlackRock, Vanguard,
  Nokomis (~24%), Needham/Lake Street/B. Riley on the sell side. **One honest wrinkle** — the *loud*
  "humanoid" branding around the company (a Micro-Measurements "Humanoid Robotics" landing page, VPG
  social posts, a "Humanoid Robotics Technology" repost account) is VPG's *own* marketing, layered on
  top of a still-small order flow. That's self-positioning, not a fraud tell, but flag it: the
  marketing volume is louder than the revenue.

Nothing trips the one hard rule. Move on — the real work here is layer two.

## 2. Which bucket? — Bucket 2 (catalyst grower, $1–5B). Barely, and *recently*.

At ~$1.67B cap it sits cleanly in the $1–5B band — past nano/fraud risk, a real profitable company on
a catalyst. But note *how it got here*: as recently as **late February 2026 this was a ~$600M company**
straddling the Bucket-1/Bucket-2 line. The 3x re-rate *moved it into Bucket 2 and consumed most of the
obvious asymmetry in the same motion.* It has Bucket 2's character — but you're meeting it after the
first leg, not before it. The brief's own bull/base/bear was framed before that distinction was clear;
the verification makes it the center of the call.

The instrument question (Section: Instrument note) pushes it toward **equity-only in practice** despite
the >$1B "LEAPS bucket" default — the chain exists but isn't really tradeable.

## 3. Bull / base / bear — equity return shape (it's effectively an equity decision; see instrument note)

**Bull (~2x from here, to a ~$3.3B EV).** Humanoid stops being a rounding error and becomes a *real*
high-margin line — call it $80–120M of sensing/tactile/force-torque revenue in 2–3 years as multiple
humanoid OEMs hit volume production. That's 20–40x management's *current* trajectory, and on a thin
~13M-share float modest real buying moves the stock hard. The market keeps a premium "physics-of-touch
picks-and-shovels" multiple (6–7x sales / 30x+ EBITDA) on a now-$400M+, growing base. This is a real
path — but it requires the ramp to *vastly exceed* both the ~$1M current order book and management's
own ~50%/yr model. You're underwriting the second leg, and it has to be a big one.

**Base (the base business carries it — and that alone doesn't justify $125).** The core industrial
recovery is genuinely underway (book-to-bill 1.21, five straight quarters >1.0). Revenue climbs back
toward the 2023 ~$355M level and EBITDA normalizes to ~$45–55M. Fair-for-quality that's ~15–20x EBITDA
→ ~$0.7–1.1B EV. **Note where that lands: *below* today's ~$1.6B EV.** A clean base-business recovery
*without heroic humanoid* implies the stock drifts *down*, not up — because the price already embeds
the humanoid call. That's the uncomfortable truth the triple created.

**Bear (the ramp slips and the story de-rates — ~40–55% downside).** Humanoid stays ~1% of revenue
with "unclear timing" (management's own words), the core recovery is gradual, and the multiple
compresses toward the peer group (~12–20x EBITDA / ~3x sales). That's ~$0.8–1.0B EV → roughly
**$55–75/share**, back toward where it traded in March–April 2026. The entire ~$44–109 analyst-target
band sits in *this* zone, not at the current price. A thin float that went up violently comes down
violently too.

## 4. What kills it — and most of it is *the price*, not the company

**The thesis-breaker: you're paying, in full, for a ramp that hasn't happened.** Humanoid was **~$4M of
$307M revenue in 2025 (~1.3%)**, ~$600K *recognized* in Q1-FY2026, and the *order book* is ~$1M.
Management models ~50%/yr growth — i.e., ~$6M in 2026, low-teens-millions by 2028. Real, but tiny for
years. Against that, the stock tripled in three months to ~63x EBITDA / ~5x sales and **trades above
its highest analyst price target ($125 vs $109).** Back out a normal industrial multiple for the
base business (~$0.9–1.0B EV) and **roughly 40–80% of today's enterprise value is humanoid optionality.**
Per the charter, "it ran" is not a reason to pass — but "genuinely overvalued on fundamentals" *is*,
and this clears that bar. This is the real finding.

**The structural ones, in order:**
- **Family voting control via a dual-class share structure.** A 10-vote **Class B** super-class
  (~7.7% of shares) hands the **Zandman/Shoshani family ~33–35% of the total vote off a
  low-single-digit economic stake**, with a family-trust agreement *contractually requiring* shares be
  voted to elect Marc Zandman and Ziv Shoshani as directors. VPG isn't a "controlled company" under
  NYSE rules (no single >50% holder, so it keeps an independent board and committees), but **public
  holders cannot force a sale or win a contested vote.** No activist unlock here.
- **Profitability is thin and was *falling* into this run.** Net income declined four straight years
  ($36M → $26M → $9.9M → $5.3M), operating margin collapsed from 11.8% (FY23) to 4.5% (FY25), and
  FY25's $5.3M net income was *flattered by a one-time $5.5M asset-sale gain.* ROE ~1.6%, ROIC ~2.4%.
  The stock tripled while the earnings base shrank.
- **The order inflection hasn't reached the P&L yet.** Q2 revenue is guided to just $85–90M (single-digit
  sequential) despite the 1.21 book-to-bill, and *EBITDA margin is going backwards* (7.0% in Q1, down
  from 11.5% two quarters earlier) because of deliberate new-org SG&A spend. Bookings are real; the
  revenue/margin breakout is still a promise.
- **Real, better-capitalized competition in humanoid sensing.** ATI Industrial Automation (owned by
  Novanta) launched a "Varo" force/torque sensor *purpose-built for humanoid limbs*; Bota Systems,
  Futek, Sensata and TE are all in the space. VPG's edge is the foil-strain-gauge *element* (the
  "secret sauce"), a genuine niche — but a component-layer moat, not a locked socket, and per-unit
  content *compresses* from $400–500 to $150–250 as volumes scale.

## The crux, made crisp: a real two-legged catalyst that the price has already collected

Both legs are genuine — verification confirmed them — so be fair to the bull case before landing the bear.

**Leg 1 — humanoid design-in is real and buyer-driven, not adjacency.** On the May 12 call management
detailed ~$600K shipped to humanoid makers in Q1, guided to "more than double" in Q2, named a **$4M
2025 baseline**, placed **two established customers in pre-production** (plus a smaller third at
prototype and a fourth "humanoid maker" in early discussions — a defense/home/industrial startup), and
at the March Sidoti conference described moving from "deep engineering collaboration… to final system
setup and early alpha builds," with "a few dozen robots, each using roughly 20–30 sensors," a follow-on
"order for hundreds of units," and "~$1.5M of orders, primarily for tactile sensing — the fingers of
the robot." Concrete sensor counts, dollar orders, named functions, customer-specific proprietary
designs: that's *real design-in*, the right direction of attribution. **The honest caveat:** every word
of it comes from VPG. No humanoid OEM has publicly named VPG (NDA-expected), and **the word "humanoid"
appears nowhere in the actual 10-Q** — it lives entirely in call commentary and IR decks. Credible, but
operator-represented and unverified by any buyer.

**Leg 2 — the base business is genuinely inflecting.** Q1-FY2026 bookings **$102.1M (+25.5% q/q),
book-to-bill 1.21 — the best since 2022**; Sensors bookings **$45.2M, a 15-quarter high** (segment B2B
1.36); the **fifth consecutive quarter of book-to-bill ≥ 1.0**. And the demand is *secular, not
restocking* — management was explicit: AI infrastructure (precision resistors for semiconductor
front/back-end test, data centers, fiber optics) and defense/space (DTS data-acquisition for hypersonic
and jet-engine test; Artemis 2, SpaceX Dragon, Blue Origin), a clean rotation away from the cyclical
general-industrial mix of 2022. (Current segments, correcting the brief: **Sensors / Weighing Solutions
/ Measurement Systems** — VPG reorganized; the old FTP/Force-Sensors/WCS names are retired.)

**So the crux is purely about price and timing.** The company is real, the catalyst is real, the base
recovery is real. What's *also* real is that the market already paid for all of it: a ~$600M company
became a ~$1.67B company in three months, on ~63x EBITDA, above the Street's highest target, while the
humanoid line is ~1% of revenue and the order inflection hasn't yet touched margins. **You are not early
to this — you'd be buying the confirmation, near highs, on a thin illiquid float, with the base case
implying downside if humanoid is merely "fine."** The bigger second leg has to be *enormous* to justify
the entry here.

## Instrument note — read before thinking about structure

**Effectively equity-only, despite being a >$1B name.** A listed chain *does* exist — 12 expirations
out to **November 2027** (~18 months), including a **January 2027 LEAPS** (no January 2028) — which is
better than SDGR, where nothing traded past Dec 2026. But **the long-dated strikes are essentially
untradeable**: verified prior-day prints show 0–5 contracts/day on Jan-2027 and Nov-2027 calls, several
ATM long strikes printing *zero* trades, and open interest/spreads that couldn't be confirmed on the
data plan but are strongly implied to be thin and wide (multi-dollar bid/ask on $30–60 premiums).
You could *leg into* a small Jan-/Nov-2027 position with patient limits and accept the friction, but
you can't size it, can't exit cleanly, and would pay a punishing spread. **Treat VPG as equity for any
meaningful position;** if you want the LEAPS, verify live OI and bid/ask in a real broker chain on the
specific strike first — that's the one number this run couldn't pull.

Two more structure facts: **the thin-float buyback amplifier is *not* live** — the repurchase
authorization lapsed in August 2024 and dividends are *contractually barred* by the credit agreement —
and **short interest is a mild amplifier, not a thesis** (~7% of float, ~1.5–2.4 days to cover, shorts
were *reducing* into the run).

### Bottom line
A real, clean, well-run niche manufacturer with a genuine two-legged catalyst — a buyer-driven humanoid
strain-gauge/force-torque design-in *and* a secular AI-infrastructure/defense order inflection (1.21
book-to-bill, best since 2022). The fraud gate is an easy pass and both legs survived verification. But
the hard DD lands on price: the stock **tripled in three months and doubled in the last month**, to
~63x EBITDA / ~5x sales, **above its highest analyst target**, while humanoid is ~1% of revenue with a
~$1M order book and the bookings haven't yet reached revenue or margins. Back out a normal multiple for
the base business and **40–80% of the enterprise value is humanoid optionality you'd be paying for in
full, after the move.** Layer on entrenched family voting control (10-vote Class B), four years of
declining earnings, and an effectively-equity-only/illiquid options chain, and the verdict is not "is
it real" (it is) but "is the entry any good here" — and near $125, after a triple, it isn't. **Respect
the setup, keep it on the watchlist, and want it ~40–50% lower** (the $55–75 zone where the base
business alone supports the price and the humanoid call comes free) — *or* on hard evidence the humanoid
ramp is dwarfing management's own model. Buying the confirmation near highs is paying top dollar to be
late.
