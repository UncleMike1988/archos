## 3 — Question 3: Does the subcontractor status actually matter?

### 3.1 Stream 4 structural counter-analysis — PRIOR DD WAS MISFRAMING THIS

**Bottom line: the prior DD's "subcontractor to Skydio" framing was a category error.** Stream 4 establishes:

**(a) SRR is open-architecture by design.** Skydio X10D explicitly: "an open, modular platform that supports third-party applications. RAS-A compliance and open MAVLINK protocol enable the use of third-party and government-owned flight application software" (Skydio public statements; Defense Advancement). The Army's RAS-A IoP doctrine treats payloads as separately-acquired items with interoperability requirements, not as bundled-into-the-platform components. AgEagle won a separate DIU contract specifically to deliver RAS-A IoP ground control — proving the Army contracts payload software separately from the airframe.

**(b) SPAI is platform-agnostic — confirmed.** SPAI describes its product as "Drone Agnostic AI Landmine Detection Capability." Confirmed integrations: Skydio X10D, Red Cat Black Widow, Ondas Holdings (MOU), Unusual Machines (MOU), Lantronix edge compute (Jan 2026 partnership). TAK/ATAK integration means SPAI detections flow to the Army's tactical network independent of which drone captured the imagery.

**(c) SPAI is already a direct subcontractor to a US Gov prime — independent of Skydio.** The Feb 2026 $1M AI Edge Processing contract was with an unnamed US Gov prime contractor. The Apr 2026 Master Teaming Agreement is with a US government prime defense contractor. **SPAI's primary contracting relationship is not through Skydio at all.** The prior DD conflated platform integration (Skydio SRR) with contracting structure (independent gov prime).

**(d) Palantir analog is valid.** PLTR rerated from $20B IPO (2020) to $200B+ (2025) — >10x — on a software-only model, originally subcontracted under ECS Federal on Project Maven, then displaced the prime as Pentagon designated PLTR sole supplier of Maven Smart System. On TITAN, PLTR is now PRIME with Northrop Grumman / Anduril / L3Harris as subs — the inversion. The pattern: **software-on-someone-else's-hardware is a beachhead, not a ceiling. The differentiated software ate the integration role.**

**(e) Anduril vertical-integration-or-die thesis does NOT apply here.** Anduril vertically integrated specifically because primes (Raytheon, Lockheed) wouldn't carry their software — primes have vendor-lock-in incentives and cost-plus economics that discourage interoperability. The prime-internalization threat bites when the host platform is a prime with proprietary software ambitions. **It does NOT bite when the host is Skydio (non-prime hardware company with open architecture by design).**

**(f) Margin economics — SPAI captures MORE per unit than Skydio.** 72% gross margin on AI software vs ~25-35% typical defense hardware. On per-X10D-deployed basis: Skydio captures $30K airframe at 30% GM = $9K gross profit; SPAI captures $5K software-license at 72% GM = $3.6K gross profit. SPAI captures ~40% of the gross profit on ~17% of the revenue. **At software-margin economics, the "subcontractor" label conflates revenue position with profit position.**

**(g) Data moat is real but eroding (24-36 month window).** 2.75M Ukraine battlefield images, 50K+ confirmed detections, 150+ landmine types, ATAK integration. Ukraine opened the dataset for partner access in March 2026 — meaning raw frames may become commodity. But SPAI's data is annotated, mission-validated, and ATAK-wired. Catch-up time for L3Harris/RTX/Chemring NIITEK is 24-36 months minimum. **The 2026-2028 window is when SPAI captures its re-rate; after which competitive pressure intensifies.**

### 3.2 Verdict on Question 3

**The prior DD's biggest concern partially dissolves.** Being a software/AI overlay on Skydio's SRR PoR is structurally STRONG, not weak — provided three conditions hold (they do for SPAI today):
- The underlying platform is open-architecture with separate payload acquisition lane ✅

**This is a material upgrade to the prior DD's Section 2 score (Counterparty Quality).
