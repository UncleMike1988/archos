The thesis conflated Sumitomo Bakelite's real #1 position in *general* EMC with the *specific* MR-MUF chokepoint, which is Namics's franchise.

(Not LOVED-EXTREME either — quality institutional name, P/E ~21, not parabolic retail.)

3. **The H8 end-market FAILS the >50% test.** Sumitomo Bakelite is a three-way diversified chemical: year-ended-March-2025 segment split was **Semiconductor Materials 33.3% / High Performance Plastics 33.0% / Quality of Life Products 33.5%** (primary source: company IR).
