## SECTION 2 — COUNTERPARTY QUALITY

### 2.1 Net Zero Innovation Hub for Data Centers — UNPACKED

**What it is:** A "technology acceleration program" headquartered in **Fredericia, Denmark**. Backers: Google, Microsoft, Data4, Vertiv, Schneider Electric, Danfoss.

**AirJoule's status:** "Selected as one of three winners, from more than seventy applicants" in September 2025. The only US-based company; the only company focused on water solutions.

**What it IS:**
- Technology validation program with testbed facility access
- Phased roadmap: engagement with DC partners → product-market fit refinement → commercial alignment → test/validation
- AirJoule will DEPLOY an AirJoule system at the Denmark testbed facility in summer 2026

**What it is NOT:**
- ❌ NOT a binding commercial agreement
- ❌ NOT a purchase order from Google, Microsoft, Vertiv, or Schneider
- ❌ NOT a revenue-generating contract
- ❌ NOT a Letter of Intent for hyperscaler deployment
- ❌ NOT exclusive — 3 winners selected, all competing for downstream commercial work

**Specific language from 10-K:** *"We anticipate deploying an AirJoule system at the program's testbed facility in Denmark in 2026."* — "anticipate" + "testbed facility" = pre-commercial validation.

**Has any of Google/Microsoft/Vertiv/Schneider/Danfoss publicly endorsed AirJoule by name?** No public statements found from those companies specifically about AirJoule. They are program sponsors of the Hub, not customers of AirJoule.

**Counterparty quality of the Hub itself:** Real consortium — Google, Microsoft, Vertiv, Schneider Electric are all $50B+ market-cap companies. The program is legitimate. **But AirJoule's RELATIONSHIP to those companies is via a competitive selection program, not a commercial contract.**

### 2.2 AirJoule Prime — status check

**Reality as of Q1 2026:**

- NOT deployed at a hyperscaler facility
- NOT independently validated by a third party in commercial environment
- Q1 2026 work: "continued assembly of the first AirJoule Prime system"
- Plan: Deploy at Innovation Hub testbed in Denmark, summer 2026

**Performance claims:** 2,000 L/day at ≤130 Wh/L using low-grade waste heat (at 80°F, 60% RH). These are TARGETED performance specs, not third-party verified.

## BONUS — TECHNOLOGY ASSESSMENT

### Underlying physics
AirJoule uses a Metal-Organic Framework (MOF) sorbent in a proprietary pressure-swing system:
1. Air is drawn through MOF-coated heat-exchanger contactors
2. Water vapor adsorbs onto MOF surface in one chamber (releases heat of adsorption)
3. Chamber doors close; vacuum is applied; heat of adsorption from chamber 1 is transferred to assist desorption in chamber 2
4. Released water vapor condenses into liquid in vacuum condenser
5. NSF-compliant materials in water-contact path produce FDA-bottled-water-quality output (PFAS-free)

**This is genuinely different from refrigerant-based AWG (condense below dew point — energy-intensive) and from desiccant-based AWG (regenerate via direct heat input — energy-intensive). The MOF + pressure-swing + heat-transfer-between-chambers architecture is the differentiator.**

### Efficiency claims (company-stated; not independently verified at hyperscale)
- AirJoule Prime: ≤130 Wh/L using low-grade waste heat at 80°F, 60% RH
- AirJoule Core: <160 Wh/L (5th-gen prototype, Q4 2024)
- **vs. refrigerant-based AWG: 400-700 Wh/L** → 3-4x more efficient
- **vs. desiccant-based AWG: 1,300+ Wh/L** → 8x more efficient
- GE Vernova Advanced Research Center "confirmed" PFAS-free distilled water output (related-party validation, not independent third-party)

### Nobel Prize signal (Oct 2025)
- 2025 Nobel Prize in Chemistry awarded to **Susumu Kitagawa, Richard Robson, and Omar Yaghi** for MOF development
- **Yaghi was a contributor to the DoD AIR2WATER program that spawned the GE Vernova JV partnership with AirJoule**
- This is a meaningful legitimacy signal for the underlying science. It does NOT validate AirJoule's specific implementation, but it validates the scientific category.
- *Note: AirJoule's specific MOF formulations and pressure-swing system architecture are patented separately and constitute the company's IP, not Yaghi's specific work.*

### IP position
