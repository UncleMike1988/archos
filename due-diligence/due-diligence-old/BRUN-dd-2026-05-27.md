## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

BRUN is a Chicago-based GPU cloud / "neocloud" provider that completed a de-SPAC
merger with Willow Lane Acquisition Corp (WLAC) on May 8, 2026. The company
markets itself as an NVIDIA Preferred Cloud Partner + Exemplar Cloud-certified
operator with **$940M of long-term contracted customer revenue**, $375M+ ARR
exit guide for FY2026, six operating data centers, and 125MW+ targeted capacity
on Blackwell architecture. The bull narrative is being built around BRUN as
"the next CoreWeave / Nebius at a sub-$2B market cap."

The DD finds **three RED scores** (Sections 1, 2, 5) and **three FLAG scores**
(Sections 3, 4, 6). Per the v2.0 hard-reject rule (3+ REDs OR any RED on Section 1
or Section 2), this is a **HARD REJECT** at this point in time. The Section 1
RED is driven by the Willow Lane sponsor's network-failure-pattern (2 of 3 prior
SPAC de-mergers DISSOLVED with zero shareholder recovery). The Section 2 RED is
the **SHAZ pattern**: $940M of contracted revenue with **exactly one named end
counterparty** (Fluidstack, $127M / 2 years) — leaving ~$813M of contracted
revenue with NO public customer disclosure. The Section 5 RED is the
**14x revenue ramp dependency** ($26.89M FY25 → $375M ARR exit FY26) layered
on top of a **going concern qualification** in the audited Boost Run Holdings
financial statements, contradicting the "FCF positive" marketing claim.

Mitigants exist — NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud is a genuine and elite certification
(peer group: CoreWeave, Nebius, Oracle, Azure); Karos's prior business (Blue
Fire Capital) was a legitimate exit to Galaxy Digital in 2020; D.A. Davidson's
$25 PT coverage is independent and not conflicted; the zero SPAC redemption
delivered $134.5M of fresh trust capital intact; and the operational model
(asset-light platform on Dell hardware in TierPoint colos with vCluster as the
Kubernetes layer) is the modern neocloud playbook. These do not overcome the
combined Section 1 + Section 2 + Section 5 REDs.

**June 3, 2026 first earnings call as a public company is the next inflection.**
What that report shows (named customers, contract type, GAAP cash flow, employee
count, Q1 actual revenue, path to $375M ARR) will determine whether BRUN
becomes investable or remains a hard reject.

**Class B is NOT supervoting.** The Pubco Amended and Restated Bylaws state:

| Block | Shares | % of 61.4M outstanding |
|---|---|---|
| Karos (Class B, all of it) | 29,533,018 | **48.1%** |
| Other Sellers (Class A) | 14,616,982 | 23.8% |

| **Karos alone** | **29,533,018** | **48.1%** |

This is not classic supervoting (10:1 dual-class à la GOOG/META) but
**effective control via the Seller block + lock-up coordination**. Public
float governance is impossible while lock-ups are in effect.
