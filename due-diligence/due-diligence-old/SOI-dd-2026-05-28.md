## Bonus — Technology Deep Dive (Photonics-SOI)

### What is Photonics-SOI?

Photonics-SOI is a specialty Silicon-on-Insulator wafer engineered specifically for silicon photonics PIC fabrication. It differs from RF-SOI / FD-SOI in three engineered dimensions:

1. **Top-silicon thickness and optical quality** — Photonics-SOI wafers have a thicker active silicon layer (typically 220-400 nm) than RF-SOI (50-145 nm) to allow waveguide formation. The top silicon must have ultra-low optical loss and minimal defect density.
2. **Buried-oxide (BOX) thickness** — Typically 2-3 μm for Photonics-SOI vs ~150 nm for FD-SOI. The thick BOX provides the optical isolation needed to confine waveguide modes.
3. **Substrate optical loss** — The handle wafer must be optically transparent at 1310nm / 1550nm wavelengths. Specialty crystal-pull and polish are required.

Soitec's Smart Cut technology — the ion-implanted hydrogen layer that splits a donor wafer to bond a thin silicon layer onto an oxidized handle wafer — is the foundational IP. Smart Cut at 300mm is the volume manufacturing technology that makes Photonics-SOI economically viable. **No other supplier has demonstrated volume 300mm photonics-grade SOI** at the yield levels TSMC / GFS / Tower require.
