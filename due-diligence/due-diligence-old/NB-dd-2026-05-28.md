## Government Equity Conversion Probability — Detailed Assessment

This was the Screen 10 thesis-driving question. The DD finds the screen thesis **materially overstated**.

### Critical fact correction
The **Lithium Americas (LAC) precedent is DOE LPO, NOT EXIM.** The 5% equity warrant stake came from DOE Loan Programs Office in exchange for $182M debt-service deferral on the existing $2.23B DOE LPO loan (announced Oct 2025).

**EXIM Bank has ZERO public precedent for taking equity warrants in critical-minerals loans:**
- PPTA $2.9B EXIM Board-approved (May 2026) — **debt-only**, no equity warrant.
- CRML $120M EXIM LOI — **debt-only** (separate from CRML's $50M DPA → ~8% equity stake which is via Commerce/DPA, NOT EXIM).
- Tanbreez, Titan Mining, other EXIM critical-minerals lane — **all debt-only**.

EXIM's product line is **export financing + Make More In America domestic loans + Supply Chain Resiliency Initiative**. None of these include equity warrants. Equity warrants are DOE LPO product (LAC, Tesla historically) or Pentagon OSC direct stakes (USAR $277M Commerce; MP $400M Pentagon preferred; Intel $8.9B Commerce/OSC).

### LAC template — what actually happened
1. DOE LPO $2.23B loan executed Mar 2024 (Biden-era).
2. Borrower distress + restructure negotiations 2025.
3. Oct 2025: DOE LPO restructures — deferred $182M debt service over 5 years in exchange for **penny warrants** = 5% LAC equity + 5% Thacker Pass JV equity. This is **DOE LPO using warrants as restructure consideration**, not a standard loan term.

### NB's actual path to equity stake — three scenarios

**Scenario A: EXIM closes as debt-only (most likely, ~50-65% probability)**.
- EXIM Board approves $800M senior secured loan, 15-year tenor, no warrants.
- Outcome: NB cap rerates per project-finance closing milestone. Magnitude: PPTA precedent is +25-50% on Board approval 8-K; NB at $791M could rerate to $1.0-1.2B = 25-50% upside.
- NB still needs ~$200M-$1.0B additional equity to fund full capex → continued dilution overhang.

**Scenario B: Pentagon OSC parallel direct equity stake (low probability, ~5-10%)**.
- Pentagon OSC (separate from EXIM) takes direct equity stake similar to USAR ($277M Commerce / 8-16%) or Intel ($8.9B / 9.9%).
- No public indication this is in progress for NB.
- Outcome if it happened: +100-300% rerate via Intel/MP-pattern.

**Scenario C: DOE LPO co-lending or refinance with warrants (low probability, ~5-10%)**.
- DOE LPO becomes involved alongside EXIM (e.g., DOE LPO takes $400M with warrants; EXIM provides $400M debt-only).
- No public indication.

**Scenario D: EXIM breaks precedent and adds warrants (very low, ~3-5%)**.
- Would require EXIM Bank policy change.
- No public indication of policy under consideration.

**Combined probability of ANY USG equity conversion within 12 months: ~15-25%** (not the ~50-75% implied by the Screen 10 thesis framing).

**Combined probability of EXIM loan closing (debt-only) within 12 months: ~50-65%**.

**Combined probability of EXIM loan + +50% rerate within 12 months: ~35-45%**.
