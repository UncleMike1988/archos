## STRUCTURAL CAVEATS FOR THIS BATCH (read first)

Clinical-stage biotech is **structurally different** from AI-infrastructure on
multiple Archos signal dimensions. The whole batch should be read with these
caveats:

1. **Revenue inflection (Dim 1):** Clinical-stage biotechs have ~zero product
   revenue. Inflection is **trial-data milestones**, not YoY revenue. The
   functional equivalent is "first positive Phase 2b/3 readout" or "first
   pivotal-trial enrollment completion."
2. **Bellwether mention (Dim 2):** "Bellwether" for biotech is Big Pharma
   licensing/acquisition partner (Roche, Merck, Pfizer, BMS, AbbVie, Lilly,
   Sanofi, AstraZeneca, Novartis, JNJ) OR FDA designation
   (Breakthrough/Fast Track/Orphan/PDUFA). NVDA/AVGO/TSMC do not apply.
3. **Government contract (Dim 3):** Functional equivalent = NIH grant /
   BARDA contract / VA contract. Most clinical-stage biotechs = N/A.
4. **Customer deposit / AR velocity (Dim 13, 14):** Structurally N/A —
   no product sales. Functional equivalent = "deferred revenue from
   milestone payments" (only relevant if licensed-out asset).
5. **Patent filings (Dim 16):** Highly relevant — biotech IS the IP. Patent
   surge in 12 months pre-move is a real signal class.
6. **Reverse split (Dim 19):** Endemic in micro-cap biotech (Nasdaq $1
   compliance). High base-rate noise; only matters in the *post-split
   recovery* pattern.
7. **Short interest (Dim 8):** Often distorted by ADR mechanics for
   foreign issuers (ABVX, NBTX).

**The signal-vs-noise question this batch is built to answer:** which of
the 25 Archos signals are *predictive* in biotech and which are pattern-noise.

### Signals where biotech is DIFFERENT from AI-infra

1. **Catalyst structure is BINARY** — Phase 3 readouts are step-function moves (success = 5-20x overnight; failure = -80%). AI-infra is more incremental on multi-quarter customer ramps. **Different timing structure for entry.**
2. **Discipline (Dim 12) interpretation flips** — In AI-infra, ATM use late in the cycle is positive (raising on strength). In biotech, ATM/RDO use *into* a catalyst can be positive (CELC raised $400M into VIKTORIA-1 strength → funded launch) OR negative (SLS, perpetual dilution). **Need to distinguish "raising on data strength" from "raising because cash is running out."**
3. **Reverse-split-recovery (Dim 19) is endemic** — Half the biotech micro-caps have done a reverse split. Without further filtering (e.g., "reverse split within 12 months AND positive catalyst within next 12 months") the signal is too noisy in biotech to be useful standalone.
4. **Specialist 13F (BVF, Frazier, Deerfield, Bain, Baker Bros) is the real biotech bellwether** — Dim 17 needs an explicit redefinition for biotech to capture this signal class.
