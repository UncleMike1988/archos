Profile summary:
- Critical Metals Corp (Nasdaq: CRML)

- Business: Rare earth elements (REE) and critical minerals. Primary asset is the Tanbreez project in Greenland — one of the world's largest known rare earth deposits.

- EXIM: $120M letter of intent for project financing

- H10-M status: DPA Title III + EXIM LOI = STRONG signal but not yet PASS (LOI is not binding, equity negotiation not closed)
- H8 tier: TIER 2 CATALYST ($1.43-1.72B)

Why CRML matters to Archos:
The US imports 100% of its rare earth permanent magnets — primarily from China. The Trump administration has explicitly made REE supply chain independence a national security priority, with direct equity investments in MP Materials ($400M preferred + 15% Pentagon stake) and USA Rare Earth ($277M + $1.3B loans). CRML's Tanbreez project in Greenland offers a non-Chinese, allied-nation source of heavy rare earth elements (dysprosium, terbium — critical for defense applications and EV motors). The $50M DPA grant with active equity negotiation puts CRML on the same progression path as MP Materials.

The risks:
- Greenland is NOT the United States. Geopolitical risk from Danish/Greenlandic sovereignty concerns.
- The project is in Greenland — Arctic construction, extreme climate, remote logistics.
- REE extraction and separation is technically difficult and capital-intensive.
- The $50M DPA + $120M EXIM LOI may not be sufficient to fund a full REE separation facility.
- Entry window is compressing (+26% WoW) — are we chasing?
