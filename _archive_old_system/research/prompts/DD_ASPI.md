ASPI was surfaced by SCAN_05 (Nuclear Supply Chain Scan, 2026-05-27) as a TIER 2 CATALYST nuclear candidate. Profile summary:

- Market cap: ~$871M (real-time verified 2026-05-27)
- Business: Isotope enrichment using proprietary Aerodynamic Separation Process (ASP) technology. Key subsidiary: Quantum Leap Energy (QLE) for HALEU (High-Assay Low-Enriched Uranium) production.
- Revenue: +475% YoY FY2025 (isotope-driven)
- H10-N status: Pending (needs NRC milestone or DOE commitment verification)
- H8 tier: TIER 2 CATALYST ($871M)
- Potential: HALEU is the fuel for next-generation SMRs. Only LEU (Centrus Energy) currently produces HALEU in the US. If ASPI/QLE can produce HALEU at scale, it becomes the second domestic source — a genuine chokepoint in the nuclear fuel supply chain.

Why ASPI matters to Archos:
Every advanced SMR design (OKLO, X-energy, Kairos, TerraPower) requires HALEU fuel. Currently, the only US source is LEU's centrifuge cascade in Piketon, Ohio — a single point of failure. DOE has explicitly identified HALEU supply as a critical bottleneck. If ASPI's aerodynamic separation technology works at commercial scale, it breaks the LEU monopoly and becomes an essential second source. The DOE has funded HALEU programs specifically to create supply redundancy.

The LEU parallel: LEU went from $189.8M deferred revenue signal to a multi-billion rerate as the nuclear renaissance thesis played out. ASPI at $871M could be the earlier-stage version — but the technology risk is much higher (ASP enrichment is unproven at commercial scale, while LEU uses proven centrifuge technology).
