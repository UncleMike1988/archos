- Market cap: ~$4.6B (real-time verified 2026-05-27)
- Business: Nuclear measurement, detection, monitoring, and safety instrumentation. Products used across commercial nuclear power, defense nuclear, medical radiation, and nuclear decommissioning.
- Q1 2026: Orders +42% (19% organic), backlog $1.1B (+38%)

- H8 tier: TIER 3 COMPOUNDER (borderline at $4.6B)

Why MIR matters to Archos:
Every SMR that gets built — regardless of reactor design (NuScale, OKLO, X-energy, Kairos, TerraPower, GE Hitachi) — needs Mirion's nuclear instrumentation and safety monitoring systems. MIR doesn't bet on which reactor wins; it supplies ALL of them. This is the "picks and shovels" thesis for the nuclear renaissance, specifically for the SMR-for-AI-data-center buildout that hyperscalers are driving.

The POWL parallel: POWL was the "picks and shovels" play for the AI data center power buildout (switchgear needed by every data center regardless of who builds it). POWL went from $230M to $11B+ (48x). MIR at $4.6B with accelerating SMR backlog could be the nuclear version — but the magnitude expectation is lower (Tier 3 = 3-10x historical, so $14-46B potential vs $4.6B current).
