### 5. ASPI — ASP Isotopes / Quantum Leap Energy (Nasdaq) · ~$0.7–1.0B · the HALEU-deconversion moonshot, eyes open

**Why now / the fire.** A textbook Bucket-1 *setup*: a bellwether naming a bottleneck
that **doesn't exist yet.** Centrus CEO Amir Vexler (Q1-2026 call, ~May 7–11): there is
**"a hole in the fuel cycle"** — commercial deconversion of enriched uranium into
advanced-reactor fuel form doesn't yet exist at scale. The fuel-cycle map is enrichment
(LEU) → **deconversion (the hole)** → fuel fabrication, and Archos Equities never mapped that
middle step. Centrus also disclosed an exploratory deconversion JV with Oklo.

**The honest read.** The *funded, government-scale* version of this hole is already
claimed: the DOE HALEU Deconversion program (up to $800M / 10 yr) was awarded back in
Oct 2024 to six incumbents — BWXT, Centrus, Framatome, GE Vernova, Orano, Westinghouse.
The remaining opportunity is *merchant/commercial* deconversion for the advanced-reactor
market. **ASPI is the listed lead, and it passes the "is it real?" test** — it's a real
company with actual (small) isotope-enrichment and radiopharma revenue (~$24M FY25), the
Renergen helium/LNG acquisition, BlackRock as a top holder, and Canaccord coverage. It
has been short-targeted and is contested, but it is *not* a hollow shell with a paid-IR
pump — it clears the one hard rule. **But its HALEU/nuclear piece is exactly the MOU
trap to respect:** Quantum Leap Energy's fuel work is framework/MOU-stage (a May-2026
MOU to *potentially* supply HALEU "beginning 2028"), not a funded award, and ASPI/QLE is
*not* one of the six DOE deconversion awardees. You'd be buying the real isotope business
plus a free option on the QLE nuclear spin-out — the QLE separate listing is the catalyst
to watch.

**Bucket.** 1 (moonshot). **Instrument.** Equity only.

**One risk.** It dilutes to fund itself and the nuclear catalyst is MOU-stage — if the
QLE spin/funding slips and isotope revenue stays small, the stock is priced on narrative.

**The honest read (the one hard rule applied).** *Contested, but legitimate — not a

This is an *option on EO-polymer adoption*, not a fraud — the burden of proof sits on the
unsigned licensing deal and four anonymous Stage-3 customers converting to revenue.

**Bucket.** 2/3 in spirit but with binary, narrative-driven risk. **Instrument.** Equity
(US-listed options exist; treat as a speculative equity option).

**One risk.** Two decades of "revenue is almost here" — if the lead-customer licensing
