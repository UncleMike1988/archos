## Framework lessons (from 3-phase research, 32 stocks)

- **H2 (analyst PT 2x revisions) is KILLED.** 0/12 leading across the combined universe; uniformly +3-6 months lagging. Never screen on PT changes. Use only as fade-confirmation — "consensus PT still at multi-year low" = setup intact.
- **H3 (insider Form 4 buying) is KILLED.** Insiders in this universe sell into rallies, not buy ahead. Founders / execs build positions through option grants over years and treat the rerate as a liquidity event. Drop Form 4 from entry screen.
- **H4 (any-fund 13F accumulation) is KILLED.** 45-day filing lag + same-quarter accumulation = strictly concurrent or lagging. Never use broad 13F screening as entry signal.
- **H7 (specialist mandate 13F) was DOWNGRADED in Phase 3.** Combined hit rate 3/12 = 25%; 0/4 in Phase 3. Treat as opportunistic upgrade only, not required. Absence does not preclude a candidate.
- **H10 has two tiers.** Vendor-level (NVDA-NBIS PIPE Dec 2024, NVDA-NVTS partnership May 2025, NVDA-LITE $2B Mar 2026) is highest conviction. Category-level (Meta/Microsoft on "power", AVGO on "networking") still works but requires H8 + H5 + H11 to narrow the candidate set.
- **H8 must be END-MARKET, not product-line.** WOLF passed product (~100% SiC) but failed end-market (>50% EV/auto, not AI infra). Tighten the wording in every screen — revenue anchored to a non-AI end-market disqualifies even on a pure product line.
- **H5 dose-response holds only when H11 passes.** WOLF was IGNORED-extreme + going-concern → -98%. BE was IGNORED-extreme + clean balance sheet → +16-30x. Always pair H5 IGNORED-extreme with the H11 check before treating as high-magnitude upgrade.
- **Bellwethers name chokepoints at CATEGORY level, not vendor level.** The H10 taxonomy is the bridge from category to specific candidate. Maintain it quarterly after each NVDA / TSMC / AVGO / MSFT / META / AMD earnings cycle.
- **Framework is for revenue-stage chokepoint pure-plays.** Pre-revenue moonshots (LWLG), sector pivots (CIFR), M&A pivots (SEI), geopolitical materials (MP, UUUU) are out of scope — parallel frameworks required if pursued.
- **Aschenbrenner's 13F is the highest-yield discovery vector.** 7 of 12 Phase 3 candidates surfaced from Situational Awareness LP holdings. Pull quarterly after 13F deadlines (2/15, 5/15, 8/15, 11/15).
- **Going-concern qualification is a hard FAIL on H11.** Do not loosen — admitting BW (going-concern → 30-49x) means admitting all going-concern legacy industrials. False-positive risk explodes.
- **Coverage initiations are 0/12 leading.** Modal pattern is +2-4 months post-inflection. Drop from entry screen entirely. Treat as exit / sizing signal at best.
- **The framework was built to REJECT the consensus AI cohort.** AMAT, KLAC, MRVL, AMD, COHR all hard-fail H8 mega-cap filter + H5 LOVED filter. 0/10 false positives in Phase 2 controls. Do not weaken these filters chasing edge cases.
- **Inflection day is often the catalyst itself.** ~50% of Phase 1+3 inflections were a Material Definitive Agreement 8-K (Item 1.01) or quarterly earnings release. H1' filing language fires concurrent (-1 to +0 weeks) more often than -16 weeks ahead.

## Known blind spots

- **Legacy-industrial-capacity-pivot (BW pattern):** 0% AI revenue pre-move, going-concern flag, repurposable manufacturing capacity. The only leading signal is the anchor-customer LOI — which IS the inflection. No filter tuning fixes this without exploding false positives. Documented, accepted limitation.
- **Sector pivot (CIFR / APLD / IREN pattern):** pre-existing legacy business with stranded assets retroactively re-narrated. Cannot screen ex ante from pre-pivot base — there's no AI thesis to attach. Different pattern, parallel framework if pursued.
- **M&A pivot (SEI pattern):** acquisition acquires the chokepoint exposure. Pre-pivot identity unrelated to AI. Catch via M&A news flow, not via 4-filter test.
- **Pre-revenue moonshot (LWLG pattern):** H8 cannot apply (no revenue); H10 fails because foundry-partner validation (Tower Semi) triggers rerate, not bellwether name-drop. High-variance asset class — exclude from core framework.
- **Geopolitical materials (MP / UUUU pattern):** non-bellwether claimants (Pentagon, Apple, China MOFCOM) drive narrative. Loosening H10 to admit these admits all critical-minerals stories (lithium, gallium, tungsten, antimony). Keep H10 strict; treat as separate framework variant (H13).
