## CONTEXT

**Scan A: Nuclear Supply Chain (beyond raw materials)**
OKLO, SMR/NuScale, and first-wave nuclear names have rerated to multi-billion. PPTA (antimony, $3.34B) and CRML (REE, $1.36B) are raw materials plays already in the Archos universe. But the nuclear buildout requires more than fuel and minerals — it needs construction services, cooling systems, instrumentation, site preparation, fuel fabrication, and balance-of-plant equipment. At the $500M-$5B tier, there should be nuclear supply chain companies that benefit from EVERY SMR deployment regardless of which reactor design wins.

The H10-N bellwether analog: NRC licensing milestone, DOE LPO commitment, or binding utility/hyperscaler offtake with disclosed MW and dollar value.

**Scan B: Hidden AI Segment Inside Mid-Cap Parents ($5-15B)**
The 2026-05-27 framework evolution added TIER 4 SEGMENT: $5-15B parent with an AI segment growing >40% YoY while the parent is flat. The market prices the parent on the blended multiple. When the AI segment crosses 50% of revenue, the multiple re-rates to the AI peer set. That crossover is the catalyst.

The SANM pattern: $13.8B parent, AI segment 3.78x YoY (but included M&A — organic was only 22%). The opportunity is finding the ORGANIC version of SANM — a mid-cap industrial with a genuine AI segment growing 40%+ organically that's being valued at the parent's boring multiple.

## TASK B: HIDDEN AI SEGMENT SCAN (45 min)

### Step 1: EdgarTools 10-Q segment search
This requires a different methodology — searching for AI-related language WITHIN segment disclosures of $5-15B parents:

- "artificial intelligence" in 10-Q filings, $5-15B market cap range (use market cap filter if available, otherwise post-filter)
- "data center" in 10-Q filings, $5-15B range
- "AI infrastructure" in 10-Q filings
- "GPU" in 10-Q filings, $5-15B range (captures companies selling to GPU/AI end-market)
- "machine learning" in 10-Q filings

For each hit: read the snippet. Is AI mentioned as a SEGMENT with disclosed revenue, or just as a buzzword in the MD&A? Only segment-level disclosure is actionable.

### Step 2: Segment-level revenue analysis
For each $5-15B company found with an AI-related segment:
- Pull segment revenue for last 4 quarters
- Calculate segment YoY growth
- Calculate segment as % of total company revenue
- Filter: segment >20% of total AND segment growing >40% YoY AND total company revenue flat or declining

The magic candidates: AI segment growing 50-100%+ while total company is flat. The market is pricing the company on the blended flat multiple. When the AI segment crosses 50% of total, the multiple re-rates.

### Step 3: Web search for "hidden AI segment" pattern
- "AI segment revenue growth mid cap 2026"
- "data center segment" "percentage of revenue" growing
- "AI business unit" "fastest growing" "$5 billion" OR "$10 billion" company
- site:seekingalpha.com "AI segment" "growing" "undervalued"
- "sum of the parts" "AI" "data center" "undervalued" 2026

### Step 4: Analyst coverage gap check
For any candidate found: how many analysts cover it? If the parent has 5-10 analysts but NONE of them are valuing the AI segment separately with a sum-of-the-parts model, that's the H5 IGNORED analog — the AI segment is hidden inside the parent's boring valuation.

### Step 5: LEAPS availability and liquidity
Tier 4 candidates ($5-15B parents) WILL have liquid LEAPS. For each candidate:
- Verify options chain exists
- Check LEAPS availability (Jan 2027, Jan 2028)
- Note bid-ask spread (should be tight at this cap level)
- The instrument here is LEAPS on the parent — you're buying the multiple re-rate as the AI segment dominates
