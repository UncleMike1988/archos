- Revenue: +26.9% YoY
- SaaS ARR: +69% YoY (cloud transition driving massive recurring revenue acceleration)
- Jan 2028 LEAPS verified with strikes $30/$35/$40
- Product: AI-driven data security and governance platform (Atlas). Protects and classifies the data that AI systems access. Automated threat detection, data classification, access control.

Why VRNS might matter for Archos:
Every AI deployment — training and inference — requires access to enterprise data. As companies deploy AI agents that autonomously access databases, file systems, and cloud storage, the attack surface explodes. Varonis is the leading platform for understanding WHO has access to WHAT data and detecting unauthorized access patterns. The thesis: AI deployment at scale is impossible without data security/governance, and Varonis is the infrastructure layer that enables safe AI adoption.

The counter-argument: Varonis is a software company, not a physical infrastructure chokepoint. Software scales infinitely — there's no supply constraint. The Archos edge comes from finding physical bottlenecks (GPUs, memory, optics, power) where demand exceeds supply. Data security doesn't have a supply constraint.

This DD must resolve that tension.

## ADDITIONAL RESEARCH

### Lens Expansion Assessment (CRITICAL)

This is the question that determines whether VRNS enters the Archos framework:

1. Is data security a CHOKEPOINT for AI deployment? Meaning: can enterprises deploy AI at scale WITHOUT solving data security/governance? If yes (they can), then VRNS is a nice-to-have, not infrastructure. If no (they can't), then VRNS is a gating dependency.

2. Is there a SUPPLY CONSTRAINT? Physical chokepoints (HBM, optics, power) have supply constraints — demand exceeds manufacturing capacity. Does data security have a supply constraint? Or can enterprises buy CrowdStrike/Microsoft Purview/Palo Alto instead of Varonis?

3. The BELLWETHER test: has any AI infrastructure bellwether (NVDA, MSFT, META, AMZN, GOOG) named data security/governance as a deployment bottleneck? If Jensen Huang has never mentioned data security as a constraint, it's probably not one.

4. The CONCENTRATION test: how many companies offer AI data security? If there are 20+ competitors, it's not a chokepoint — it's a competitive market. If Varonis is 1 of 2-3 companies with the capability, it's concentrated enough to qualify.
