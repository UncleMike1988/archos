- Product: AI-powered observability platform for cloud and AI infrastructure. Monitors the performance, reliability, and security of applications running on cloud/hybrid infrastructure. Davis AI engine provides automated root cause analysis.

Why DT might matter for Archos:
Every AI workload running in production — inference endpoints, training jobs, RAG pipelines, agent frameworks — needs to be monitored. If an AI service goes down or degrades, the enterprise needs to know instantly and understand why. Dynatrace is the leading full-stack observability platform that monitors these workloads. The thesis: as AI deployments scale from experimental to production at every enterprise, the demand for AI-workload observability scales linearly. Dynatrace is the "monitoring layer" of AI infrastructure.

The counter-argument: Same as VRNS — observability is software, not physical infrastructure. It scales infinitely. There's no supply constraint. And Dynatrace competes with Datadog (DDOG), Splunk (now Cisco), New Relic, Grafana, and others. It's a competitive market, not a chokepoint.

At $11.35B and 49.93% IV, DT would be a TIER 4 SEGMENT play (H8 tiered: $5-15B parent) with the cheapest LEAPS in the entire AI-adjacent universe.
