## THE HUNT — what Bucket 2 looks for
Real companies, past the nano/fraud risk, that could do 2-5x in 18 months on a catalyst. This is the
bellwether-constraint + supply-chain hunt (the method that produced the 2026-05-29 hunt list):
- Mine the freshest bellwether earnings calls for the "we're constrained on X / demand outpaces supply
  of X" sentence — that sentence IS the chokepoint. Map it to the smallest real pure-play that makes X.
- Hunt one layer DOWN the supply chain from an obvious winner (e.g. optical transceivers → the FAU/InP/
  laser-fab/packaging layer beneath them). The undiscovered supplier-to-the-winner is the target.
- Follow smart capital (credible funds, NVDA strategic stakes, government equity) into names that haven't popped yet.
- Catch fresh catalysts: a first hyperscaler/government contract, a capacity-ramp, a first profitable quarter.

Pull the 3-5 most important/recent bellwether prints (NVIDIA, the hyperscalers, TSMC/AVGO/Vertiv/optical
names, etc.) for THIS week's constraint language, and check what's changed since last week's run.

## FOR EACH INTERESTING NAME
- The constraint / catalyst / why-now (attribution: a real buyer or a named bellwether constraint — not self-positioning).
- Bull case: the 2-5x path; which bucket; how much runway is left even if it's already moved.
- Is it real? (fraud gate). Then the hard DD: valuation (multiples — a name at, say, 40x sales is a real
  concern even if great; say so), balance sheet, growth trajectory/deceleration, margins, customer
  concentration, catalyst durability. "It ran" is fine; "it's genuinely overvalued" is a real bear point — name it.
- Instrument: is there an affordable, liquid LEAPS chain (check IV + expiries), or is it equity-only?
- One honest risk line.
