## Tool-stack lessons (new this run)

- **Live web-search market cap verification was load-bearing.** EdgarTools XBRL mkt cap remained stale and unreliable (confirmed for the Nth time). Every candidate's cap was verified via stockanalysis.com / stocktitan.net / Yahoo Finance live data.
- **The 10-K AI-keyword search returned a HUGE first-pass surface (~10,000 snippets across 10 queries).** Discrimination required aggressive triage on snippet language: "AI is the product" vs "AI as marketing buzzword." The marketing-buzzword class is the modal failure mode in this universe — every SaaS company writes "AI-powered" somewhere in their 10-K now.
- **8-K gov-contract + AI intersection produced the highest hit-to-candidate ratio.** Stream 2 surveyed ~1,800 8-Ks across 8+ queries and yielded 5 high-quality candidates — a clean ratio. Stream 1 surveyed ~10,000 10-K snippets and yielded 7 — much noisier corpus. **For nano-cap AI discovery, 8-K gov-contract intersection is the highest-yield query path.**
- **Cluster 8 pattern density is HIGHER in the sub-$100M universe than in the sub-$500M master-screen universe.** Penny-stock rename pivots, foreign Cayman/HK shells, AGPU-class de-SPAC residuals all concentrate at the bottom of the cap distribution. Aggressive Cluster 8 filtering is the difference between a useful TIER 1 list and a noise generator.
- **Cross-stream confluence is even more valuable at nano-cap than at master-screen scale.** Three names double-fired (SPAI, MSAI, KSCP). All three are credible. The single-stream candidates that DID NOT double-fire required individual DD assessment to separate the AISP-shape from the SHAZ-shape (DFNS, XTIA, ONMD all show this tension).
