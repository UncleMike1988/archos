## Structural findings

### 1. Consolidation is closing the discovery window

Marvell took Polariton (Apr 2026) + Celestial AI ($5.5B, Dec 2025). Credo took DustPhotonics ($1.3B, Apr 2026). NVIDIA invested $2B in Coherent + $2B in Lumentum (Mar 2026). Corning got a 10x capacity expansion order (NVDA). **The AAOI/CRDO/MRVL/COHR mid-cap layer is eating the next-gen photonics startup layer faster than the 800G cycle's discovery window allowed.**

For Archos Equities, this means: the window to discover sub-$5B 1.6T pure-plays BEFORE acquisition or upward rerate is materially narrower than the 800G cycle. The pattern is closer to "watch acquisition flow" than "watch 10-K language emergence."

### 2. The H10 fires at sub-$5B are rare — bellwether spend is concentrated at mega-cap

Recent bellwether-level partnerships are all >$5B:
- NVIDIA + Coherent: $2B investment + multi-year supply (COHR $73B)
- NVIDIA + Lumentum: $2B investment (LITE $69B)
- NVIDIA + Corning: 10x capacity expansion (GLW mega-cap)
- NVIDIA + Tower Semi: 1.6T SiPho partnership (TSEM $32B)
- Broadcom Tomahawk 6 Davisson 102.4 Tbps CPO Ethernet switch (shipping NOW — AVGO mega-cap)

**Zero canonical-bellwether vendor-level H10 fires at sub-$5B US-listed optical names in last 90 days.** This is consistent with the universal-cohort finding that the H10 sub-$5B fire is the rare and most-actionable signal.

### 3. "1.6T transceiver" is not yet adopted disclosure language

Phase 1 EdgarTools 18-month full-text search:
- "1.6 terabit" — multiple hits (LWLG, AAOI, CRDO context)
- **"1.6T transceiver" — ZERO 10-K hits**
- **"CPO module" — ZERO 10-K hits** (CPO described as architecture, not yet productized line item)
- "EML laser" — ZERO 10-K hits (referenced as "EMLs" / "EML chips")
- "Tomahawk 6" — only 1 hit
- Most 1.6T 10-K mentions are forward-looking language ("emerging," "expect to enable") — few firm design wins yet

**This confirms we are at the AAOI-style discovery window for 1.6T: bellwethers are signaling, mid-caps are M&A-acquiring the next layer, but 10-K disclosure language hasn't crystallized. The next 2-3 quarters of 10-K filings will be where firm 1.6T design-win language appears in sub-$5B filings.**

### 4. Quantum-dot-on-Si is an architecturally distinct bet on EML disruption

ALMU (QD-on-Si large-substrate platform) + IQE/Quintessent (QD epiwafer supply chain) are the public-market plays on this architecture bet. If QD-on-Si scales, the EML lock that LITE has at 1.6T weakens.

**Both names carry handicaps:** ALMU is on DD REJECT-track per existing record (insider issues, pre-revenue scale); IQE is LSE-listed. **No clean US-investable QD-on-Si pure-play surfaced this scan.**

### 5. AEC competitor universe under CRDO is essentially empty at sub-$5B

CRDO has ~73% AEC market share. Astera Labs is the only direct AEC competitor, and is firmly above $5B. **The H8 sub-$5B slot for AECs has no public discovery candidate currently visible.**

## Lessons captured (for INSIGHTS.md addendum if ratified by Sounding Board)

1. **Consolidation-by-mid-cap is the dominant signal for next-gen optical at sub-$5B.** When the bellwether (NVDA) writes $2B+ checks to the existing mid-cap layer (COHR, LITE) AND those mid-caps simultaneously M&A-acquire the private startup pipeline (CRDO→DustPhotonics, MRVL→Celestial+Polariton), the sub-$5B pure-play window does not have time to emerge organically. Lesson: when consolidation flow exceeds organic discovery flow, the chokepoint may go EMPTY structurally, not transiently.

2. **Pre-revenue moonshot blind spot continues to hold (LWLG case 4).** LWLG fires on multiple chokepoint-language queries (1.6T, 200G per lane, SiPh foundry partnership) but H8 cannot apply at $0.24M revenue. Framework correctly hesitates. Calibration confirmation, not a new finding.

3. **Disclosure-language adoption lags vendor activity by 1-2 quarters.** "1.6T transceiver" returns zero 10-K hits despite 1.6T being in active vendor qualification and bellwether-named in March-May 2026. The 10-K discovery cycle for this chokepoint will fire later than the bellwether-mention cycle. Implication: watch 8-K item 1.01 / 7.01 disclosures more than 10-K language for the next 2 quarters.

4. **Foreign-listed photonics names are now a 5-name cohort.** SIVEF (Sweden, already WATCH), 3778.T (Tokyo, already WATCH), Browave (Taiwan, surfaced this scan), FOCI (Taiwan, surfaced this scan), IQE.L (UK, surfaced this scan).
