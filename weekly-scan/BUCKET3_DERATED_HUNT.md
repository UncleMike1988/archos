## THE HUNT — what Bucket 3 looks for
Quality names (up to ~$200B) that pulled back on a fear that didn't materialize while revenue and earnings
kept climbing — the NOW/CRM/VEEV shape. The 2.5-3.5x comes from a multiple re-rate on an 18-month LEAPS
(often sold ~12 months in with meat left), so the setup needs: real de-rate (down meaningfully from highs),
fundamentals still compounding (revenue + earnings up, not a value trap), and low-enough IV that a LEAPS
chain is affordable and clears 2x at a reasonable target.

Hunt sources this week:
- Quality names down ~25-45% from 52-wk highs while still growing revenue/earnings (the fear-didn't-

  fundamentals contradict.

## FOR EACH INTERESTING NAME
- Why now: what fear de-rated it, and the evidence the fear didn't materialize (revenue/earnings still climbing).
- Bull/base/bear with rough return shape; confirm Bucket 3.
- Is it real / is it a value trap? The key hard-DD question here is VALUATION and DETERIORATION: is the
  de-rate an overreaction (opportunity) or deserved (business actually worse)? Multiples, growth durability,

- THE LEAPS MATH (this bucket's core deliverable): current IV, available expiries, and roughly what move

  or no long-dated chain exists, say so and treat it as equity. Show the breakeven logic plainly.
- One honest risk line (usually: the de-rate was deserved, or multiple compression continues).
