## THE HUNT — what Bucket 1 looks for
Sub-$1B / nano-cap moonshots: real product/team/clean balance sheet, ignored, just before or just into
a catalyst, with 10x asymmetry. The recurring winner shapes (from PATTERNS_AND_TRAPS) are the hunting
ground:
- A real first contract / first big customer / first government award landing on a tiny, ignored name.
- A fresh start (spinoff, post-bankruptcy emergence, restructuring + new CEO/CFO) before the re-rate.
- A beaten-down survivor that pivoted/in-licensed and a second catalyst is surprising a market that wrote it off.
- A balance-sheet demand signal (customer deposits / deferred revenue building) BEFORE it shows in revenue.
- A serious operator or senior defense/gov figure joining a tiny board with equity skin in the game.

Hunt sources this week (use web search + EdgarTools-style filing search where available):
- Fresh 8-Ks (last ~7-10 days): material agreements, first contracts, government awards at sub-$1B names.
- Recent 10-Qs (last ~30-60 days): first-time revenue inflection or first-time deferred-revenue build at nano caps.
- Fresh-start / spinoff / emergence news (last 6-18 months) not yet re-rated.
- The freshest bellwether/government constraint sentences that map to a TINY pure-play (the BE-from-one-
  Zuckerberg-sentence shape). Cross-check anything you find against the current chokepoints in play.

## FOR EACH INTERESTING NAME
- The catalyst / why-now (with attribution — a real buyer naming the company, not the company claiming adjacency).
- The bull case: the 10x path, in plain terms. Which bucket (confirm it's really Bucket 1).
- Is it real? (the fraud gate — real product, named paying customers not LOIs, clean-enough balance sheet,
  credible non-promoter operators, organic vs paid-IR). If hollow + bought story, stop and say so.
- Then the hard look: balance-sheet/dilution risk, cash runway, is the catalyst durable, insider behavior.
- Instrument: almost always equity-only at this size — say so; only note options if a real chain exists.
- One honest risk line.
