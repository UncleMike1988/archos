## INTENT FOR PHASE 2

Each stock is to be tested against 25 signal dimensions:

**Obvious (6):** revenue inflection, bellwether mention, gov contract, insider buying cluster, analyst coverage initiation, sentiment pre-move
**Unorthodox (14):** float %, short interest trajectory, auditor change, spinoff/restructuring, CFO/CEO replacement, unused shelf, customer deposit spike, AR velocity, new risk factor, patent acceleration, first 13F appearance (Vanguard/BlackRock/State Street), options chain creation/LEAPS, reverse split recovery, hiring ramp
