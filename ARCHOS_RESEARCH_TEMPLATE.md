## Thread template (X/Twitter)

Each DD thread follows this structure. Keep each tweet under
280 chars. Thread length: 6-8 tweets max.

### Tweet 1 — Hook + Verdict
Format: "ARCHOS DD: $TICKER — [VERDICT]"
One sentence on what the company claims to be.
One sentence on what we found.
Must be attention-grabbing and honest.

Example:
"ARCHOS DD: $VELO — REJECT

Velo3D claims to be SpaceX's critical 3D printing supplier.

We downloaded SpaceX's S-1 (filed 3 days ago) and grep'd it.
Zero mentions of Velo3D. SpaceX brags about building parts
in-house. The thesis is dead."

### Tweet 2 — What the company says (the bull case)
Steelman the bull case in 1-2 sentences.
What FinTwit is promoting. What the press releases say.

### Tweet 3 — What we found (the critical finding)
The single most important DD finding.
Sourced to a specific filing, document, or data point.
This is the "value" tweet — the thing that saves people money.

### Tweet 4 — The numbers (raw math)
One key ratio computed from raw data.
Show the math. "Market cap $687M on $46M revenue = 14.9x P/S.
525x dilution from two reverse splits. Going concern in the 10-K."

### Tweet 5 — Management check
One sentence on CEO history.
One sentence on insider activity (buying or selling?).
One sentence on any related-party transactions.

### Tweet 6 — The verdict explained
Why we're passing or proceeding.
What would change our mind.
Link to full report on Substack.

### Tweet 7 (optional) — Comparison
Compare to a similar stock that passed DD.
"Compare $VELO (REJECT) to $AMPG (PROCEED WITH CAUTION):
both are nano-caps with big-name associations. The difference
is AMPG's counterparty (Telus, $20B revenue) can actually pay."

### Tweet 8 — Disclaimer + CTA
"This is AI-powered research, not financial advice. Full DD
methodology and report at [Substack link]. Built on Claude by
@[your handle]."

## Rules

1. Never recommend buying or selling. Present findings, state verdict.
2. Always disclose positions. If we own it, say so.
3. Always source claims to specific filings with accession numbers or URLs.
4. Compute every ratio from raw data. Never cite third-party calculations.
5. Steelman the bull case before presenting bear findings.
6. Be transparent about being AI-powered. It's a feature, not a bug.
7. When we're wrong, post the correction publicly with the same prominence.
