## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

WhiteFiber (WYFI) is a 9-month-old IPO (Aug 7, 2025) carved out of Bit Digital (BTBT) that operates a Tier 3 data-center / GPU-cloud business across three live sites (Iceland, Montreal MTL-3, North Carolina NC-1 in construction). The stock rerated +74% in 4 weeks (~$15-17 → $30) on the back of two binding contracts — Nscale $865M / 10-year colocation (Dec 18, 2025) and an unnamed "investment-grade technology customer" Paris $160M+ / 5-year GPU compute (May 21, 2026). H5 has transitioned IGNORED → PARTIAL; the asymmetric window is narrowing.

This Code DD finds **WYFI is NOT a SHAZ-pattern reject**, but it carries **four material risks the four-filter screen did not capture**:

1. **NC-1 Phase 1 billing missed its April 30, 2026 target.** Disclosed honestly in Q1 earnings call (May 14): supply-chain issue on medium-voltage switchgear; revenue ramps Q2–Q3 instead of one-shot Phase 1 + Phase 2. NOT a contract failure, but a 1-2 quarter slip on the flagship contract that the market has not yet processed.
2. **The Paris counterparty is UNNAMED.** This is the SHAZ/ESDS risk shape — but every other contract structure detail (12-month customer prepayment, third-party DC capacity in France, project-level financing via binding term sheet) reads as legitimate enterprise procurement. Plausible candidates: Mistral AI, Iliad/Free, OVH/Scaleway, Schneider Electric, or a sovereign EU entity. Until the customer is named or until 12 months of prepayments hit the cash flow statement, this remains a FLAG-not-RED.
3. **Bit Digital holds ~70% of WYFI shares (not 33% as in the prior Sounding Board note).** The voluntary no-sell pledge through 2026 is a $810M+ commitment with NO contractual enforcement mechanism. If BTBT faces a 2027 cash crunch, the overhang is severe.
4. **Sam Tabar's 2014 personal civil lawsuit is real but unresolved publicly.** Disorderly conduct plea 2013 is the only conviction. NY Bar lapsed-then-reinstated. The "siphoning funds" allegations the prior Sounding Board flagged originate from reputation attack sites (cybercriminal.com, intelligenceline.com, financescam.com), NOT primary sources or SEC filings. He is double-dipping on comp (CEO of both BTBT and WYFI). This is a FLAG, not the SHAZ-Manning-pattern RED.
