### 1.4 Share structure — SUPERVOTING CONFIRMED (manual DD missed this)

**Source:** IREN DEF 14A filed October 6, 2025
([SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000114036125037383/0001140361-25-037383-index.htm))

**Findings:**
- IREN has a dual-class share structure: Ordinary Shares (1 vote each) and B Class
  Shares.
- **Each B Class Share confers 15 votes for each ordinary share held by the
  holder.** Only TWO B Class Shares exist.
- Daniel Roberts owns 1 B Class Share (held via Awassi Capital Holdings 2 Pty Ltd).
- William Roberts owns 1 B Class Share (held via Awassi Capital Holdings 1 Pty Ltd).

  ownership** (~9.5x voting leverage).

**Comparison to SHAZ:** SHAZ had 65% votes / 1% economics = 65x leverage.
IREN at 9.5x leverage is meaningfully less extreme — but it IS a supervoting
structure with founder-controlled voting bloc. Manual DD was wrong to say "No
supervoting found."

**Trajectory:** At IPO (Nov 2021), the Roberts brothers held 78.0% of voting power
combined. The decline to 43.6% reflects dilution from new ordinary share issuance.
On current trajectory (continued ATM + convertible conversions), voting power
will continue to dilute toward the 30%-low-40s range.
