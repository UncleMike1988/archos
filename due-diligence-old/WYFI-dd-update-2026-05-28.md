**Why Dassault is the highest-probability candidate:**

3. **Three-continents NVIDIA partnership (Feb 3, 2026):** Per [NVIDIA newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/dassault-systemes-nvidia-industrial-ai), "OUTSCALE AI factories will harness the latest NVIDIA AI infrastructure on three continents." 5. **Timing match:** Dassault-NVIDIA partnership announced Feb 3, 2026 (3 months before WYFI 8-K) — consistent with a procurement-to-binding-agreement cycle.

6. **Sovereignty requirements:** Dassault explicitly cites "data privacy, intellectual property protection and sovereignty of Dassault Systèmes' customers" — which would prefer third-party DC partner (WYFI) over building own DC.

7. **Scale match:** $32M/yr × 5 yr = $160M+ total. Dassault FY2025 revenue was €5.97B (~$6.4B). $32M/yr is ~0.5% of revenue — a reasonable single-DC procurement for an enterprise software company at this scale.

**Caveats that prevent confirmation:**
- Dassault has not announced WhiteFiber publicly.
- Dassault has its own Outscale DCs in France (3 sites: Paris, Saint-Cloud, Vélizy area) — third-party WYFI capacity would be INCREMENTAL, not replacement.

## NEW STRUCTURAL FINDINGS NOT IN PRIOR DD

### N.1 8-K Item filing-form choice (Item 7.01 vs 1.01) — YELLOW FLAG

The Paris 8-K was filed under **Items 7.01 (Regulation FD Disclosure) + 9.01 (Financial Statements and Exhibits) ONLY**, not Item 1.01 (Material Definitive Agreement).

This contrasts with:
- **Dec 18, 2025 Nscale 8-K** — filed under **Items 1.01 + 7.01 + 9.01** (material definitive agreement).
- **Jan 26, 2026 Convert 8-K (item-laden)** — filed under **Items 1.01 + 2.03 + 3.02 + 8.01 + 9.01**.

**Interpretations:**

1. **Bullish read:** The Paris agreement is binding but contingent on financing close (June 2026) and equipment acceptance. WYFI's counsel chose voluntary Reg FD disclosure to retain optionality. An Item 1.01 amendment will be filed when financing closes and contingencies are resolved.

2. **Bearish read:** The Paris "agreement" may be at term-sheet stage on the customer side as well, not just the financing side. The "binding term sheet for project-level financing" language is contractual, but the customer agreement language ("subject to final equipment delivery and acceptance milestones") creates material outs.

3. **Neutral read:** Item 7.01 voluntary disclosures are common for forward-looking material information that does not strictly meet Item 1.01 criteria. Many companies use Item 7.01 for press releases that announce signed agreements without filing the agreement as an exhibit.

**Action item:** Monitor for a follow-up 8-K filed under Item 1.01 after the June 2026 financing close. If no Item 1.01 amendment is filed when the financing closes, this is a heightened FLAG. If an Item 1.01 amendment with the actual customer agreement exhibit is filed in June-July 2026, this resolves the question.
